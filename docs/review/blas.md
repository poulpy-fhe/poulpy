# poulpy-blas review rules

Read with `docs/review/REVIEW.md`; this file adds the rules specific to `poulpy-blas`.

## 0. Terms

- **Grid, matrix slot, batch.** A grid (`BlasGrid`, `BlasCiphertext`) is `rows x cols` CKKS blocks of `d x d`, `d = 2^log_d`; a block of `S` slots holds `S / d^2` matrix slots, and the batch record `mat_slots` counts the matrices, a count the blocks' label (`log_sparsity`) must allow.
- **Plan, context, public operand.** `BlasPlan` fixes `log_d`, the map schedules and the public-product path (`BlasPtAlgorithm`); `BlasContext` holds its prepared maps (sigma, tau, the transpose, shifts phi^k and psi^k, their halves and replicated forms); `BlasPtOperand` holds public matrices encoded for one product side.
- **Map hook, JKLS kernel.** The internal OEP methods: `blas_map_blocks` evaluates maps over blocks; `blas_jkls_block` computes one output block of a ciphertext product (two encrypted grids) from prepared term pairs.
- **Terms, units, hot and cold.** A block's JKLS terms are its sigma (tau) result and that result's `d - 1` shifts, a unit the terms of one block row of `a` or column of `b`; a hot operand (`PrepareHint::OneShot`) copies its source, whose terms the product makes, a cold one (`Reuse`) holds every term transformed once.

## 1. Layering and families

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 1 and 3.

- **Families and their files.** An operation lives in its family's file (`inter`: whole matrices across matrix slots, `intra`: one map on every matrix, `matmul`: products) under `api/`, `delegates/`, `oep/`, `oep/derived/` and `reference/` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 3; JKLS kernel: `oep/jkls.rs`, `reference/jkls.rs`), with:
  - its validator and key-use enumerator in `layouts/validate.rs`;
  - its key-use, output-layout and radix queries in `layouts/planning.rs`, re-exported from `layouts`, and the layout queries a validator or `oep::derived` needs (operand, term) in `layouts`;
  - its provided body in `oep/derived/<family>.rs`, its tests in `test_suite/<family>.rs` and the cross-cutting suites (`context.rs`, `keys.rs`, `meta.rs`, `parity.rs`, `conjugate_invariant.rs`).
- **What a new operation needs.** Beyond `docs/review/workspace.md` section 3, a new `blas_<op>` brings `validate_blas_<op>` with a key-use enumerator, `blas_<op>_key_uses`, `blas_<op>_output_layout` (grid outputs), a client-side key set when it uses rotation keys, `blas_<op>_max_base2k` (products, the prepared product sharing `blas_matmul_max_base2k`), `# Metadata` and `# Errors` docs, `blas-contracts.md` rows and tests (sections 3, 4, 7, 10 and 11).
- **New families are wired everywhere.** A new OEP family adds, in the same diff, its `impl_<family>_reference_full!` to `impl_blas_reference_full!`, its trait to the supertraits and blanket impl of `BlasImpl` (its only impl, the bound every suite takes), and its rows to the Families and Reference implementations tables of `poulpy-blas/docs/blas-contracts.md` and the backend-integration paragraph of `poulpy-blas/README.md`.
- **Hooks reach every operation.** Map evaluations a family contract routes through the hook are `BE::blas_map_blocks` calls and ciphertext-product output blocks `BE::blas_jkls_block` calls, in provided bodies and references alike; products by public matrices hook only the operand's first map (`Jkls`, masked paths) and the context's masks (`JklsStored`, `JklsGalois`), `Diagonal` none.
- **Provided bodies call `oep::derived`.** A provided method's body is one `blas_<op>_derived` call and its query one `blas_<op>_tmp_bytes_derived` call (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 14).
- **CKKS families are supertraits.** A blas OEP trait takes as supertraits every poulpy-ckks OEP family its provided bodies, or the factories they use, call (`BlasMatIntraImpl: Backend + CKKSCopyImpl + CKKSAddImpl`), never a per-method where-clause.
- **Compose poulpy-ckks through its API.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 3.1, references and provided bodies compute only through blas OEP methods and poulpy-ckks API traits (through supertraits in provided bodies, per-method bounds in references), using core and hal traits only for layouts, sizes, allocation, key lookups and the bounds poulpy-ckks methods require (`GLWEMulPreparedPlainSum`); the linear-transformation encoders (`ckks_encode_linear_transformation_into`, `ckks_encode_linear_transformation_tmp_bytes`) are reached outside poulpy-ckks's `reference` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 3).
- **No `crate::reference` outside the macros.** `layouts`, `api`, `delegates` and `oep` (derived bodies included) name `crate::reference` only in opt-in macro bodies and rustdoc links, and helpers both sides need live in `layouts` or `oep::derived` (`KeyLayouts`, `assert_maps_scratch`), imported by `reference`.
- **Selection macros.** Macro-generated methods forward exactly the required methods to `$crate::reference::<Trait>::<method>_reference(module, ..)` with the exact OEP signature, inheriting every provided default (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 3), and a new required method joins its macro in the same diff.
- **Macro expansion crates.** Selection macros expand to `::poulpy_hal`, `::poulpy_core` and `::poulpy_ckks`, test registration macros to `::poulpy_hal` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 14), and a macro naming a further crate updates the README and contracts-doc dependency statements.
- **OEP contract docs.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 3 (OEP contract docs), each OEP method's and hook's doc lists the reproduced circuit under **Fixed** (or in circuit prose), bit-identical freedoms under **Free**, anything else changing bits, and a hook's caller preconditions, the family `# Safety` citing `poulpy-hal/docs/backend_safety_contract.md` and the obligations its methods share, both changing in the same diff as the circuit, schedule, key widths, error order or freedoms.

## 2. Naming and signatures

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` section 12 (blas layer names: table 12.1).

- **Layer names.** Beyond `docs/review/workspace.md` table 12.1, hooks are `blas_<hook>` with `blas_<hook>_tmp_bytes`, and the JKLS kernel, the one family outside `BlasMat<Family>`, is `BlasJklsImpl` with `impl_blas_jkls_reference_full!` and `BlasJklsReference::blas_jkls_block_reference`.
- **Argument order.** Operations take `([r | k], res, inputs in product order, [ctx], [tsk], keys, scratch)` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 12; inter operations, `blas_rotate` and `blas_sum`, without `ctx`), queries the same with key layouts and without amount and scratch, a preparation's query `(source, ctx, keys, hint)`; validators and key-use queries take `module` first, output-layout and radix queries none.
- **Sides in the type.** Sides are types (`BlasPtOperand<BE, S: BlasPtSide>` with `Right` or `Left`, `BlasMatMulLeft<BE>`, `BlasMatMulRight<BE>`), and arguments follow the product in every layer, validator and query: `blas_matmul_pt_right(res, a, b, ..)` computes `A B`, `blas_matmul_pt_left(res, b, a, ..)` computes `B A`.
- **Generic grids, thread bounds.** Methods and hooks take `R: BlasGridMut<BE>` for `res`, `A: BlasGrid<BE>` for ciphertext inputs (never `BlasCiphertext`), `K: GetAutomorphismKey<BE> + Sync`, `T: GetTensorKey<BE> + Sync`, `Sync` hook sources, kernel pairs and keys, `Send` hook destinations and `Send + Sync` prepared operands.

## 3. Validation and errors

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` section 4, including its blas error-model row.

- **Public metadata-only validators.** Every operation has a public `validate_blas_<op><BE: Backend>(module: &Module<BE>, [r | k], res, inputs.., [ctx], [tsk], keys)` in `layouts/validate.rs`, re-exported from `layouts`, returning `CKKSResult<usize>` (the batch to record) or, for a preparation with `res: &impl BlasMatMulOperandInfos`, `CKKSResult<()>`, and reading metadata only (layouts, labels, records, map shapes, key lookups), no digit or CKKS budget.
- **Validate, assert, work, stamp.** Every implementation of a grid operation (reference, provided body, override) starts with `let batch = validate_blas_<op>(module, &*res, ..)?;`, then its scratch assert (exceptions: section 8), the work and `res.set_mat_slots(batch); Ok(())`; a preparation validates first and, after filling, records its source's `blas_matmul_{left,right}_operand_layout` (under its `ctx` and hint) and `grid_batch::<BE::Ring>`, through `set_prepared` outside the crate.
- **Validators cover every input.** Validators pass each input they read or write through its kind's shared `ensure_*` helper in `layouts/validate.rs`, a new kind adding one: grids through `ensure_grid::<BE::Ring>`, `ensure_shape`, `ensure_inner` and `ensure_layouts`, contexts and public operands through `ensure_maps` (masks: `ensure_masks`), a preparation's operand through `ensure_allocated`, a prepared one through `ensure_operand` (hot: also `ensure_hot_context`).
- **Hooks and kernels trust callers.** Operations validate before entering the map hook or JKLS kernel and enter them exactly as the reference composes them; on accepted inputs these assert only their scratch (the hook also `dst.len() == src.len() * lts.len()` with at least one map, the kernel leaving `lhs.len() == rhs.len() >= 1` to poulpy-ckks) and return only poulpy-ckks's budget errors.
- **No asserts in accessors.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 4 (hot paths), crate-internal accessors (`OperandMaps::at`, `at_for`, `BlasPtOperand::plaintexts_at`) and per-block loops hold no assert, and public accessors such as `BlasCiphertext::block` assert bounds once per call.
- **Error model.** Operations, `blas_matmul_prepare_{left,right}` included, return `poulpy_ckks::CKKSResult` (imported `as Result`) with `CKKSCompositionError` variants, never `ckks_ensure!` or `ckks_bail!`, and panic only on scratch and on invariants their validator guarantees; encodings (`*_encode`) return poulpy-ckks's errors, and `blas_context_prepare` and `blas_pt_operand_prepare` panic on misuse.
- **Fixed error order.** Every implementation returns the reference's errors in its order, before any write to `res`:
  - `InvalidOperand` for structure: grid, tiling, block layout, label against batch, shape, inner dimension, degree, radix, rank, map encoding, counts, a product radix above 62;
  - keys, rotation keys in enumeration order then the tensor key, at their use widths: `MissingAutomorphismKey`, `MissingRelinearizationKey`, `InvalidOperand` for another rank;
  - poulpy-ckks's budget errors: left terms, right terms, then the first block's ct x ct rule.
- **Budget errors pass through.** poulpy-ckks's budget errors (`MultiplicationPrecisionUnderflow`, `InsufficientHomomorphicCapacity`) surface as raised, naming the poulpy-ckks operation, and, in place of the pre-check of `docs/review/workspace.md` section 4 (compositions pre-check inner calls), the first call that can raise one (the first block's, a hot side's first unit) precedes every write to `res`.
- **Rejections and their reasons.** A rejection is `InvalidOperand { op, reason }` from `layouts::validate::reject(op, reason)` or an `ensure_*` helper, `op` the public operation name (`"blas_rotate"`) used verbatim by validator, scratch assertion and tests, `reason` naming operand and requirement in the `docs/review/workspace.md` section 4 message style, without an `<op>:` prefix.
- **Pinned reasons, shared messages.** Suites match the error kind and `op`, only `validate.rs` helper unit tests pin reasons, and a condition both a query panic and a validator report shares one `pub(crate) const` message (`MASKLESS`) that tests match.

## 4. Keys and planning queries

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 4 (checked key lookups).

- **One key-use enumerator.** Rotation-key uses come from one `pub(crate)` enumerator in `layouts/validate.rs` taking a `UseSink<'_>` (yielding `RotationUse { rotation, k, rank }` in check order through `lt_uses` and `term_uses`, stopping at the first error) and the tensor width from one function (`matmul_tensor`, `prepared_tensor`), shared by the validator, `blas_<op>_key_uses` (`BlasKeyUses::collect`) and the reference's `KeyLayouts` sizing.
- **Keys at the width used.** Keys are looked up (`ensure_rotation`, `ensure_tensor_key`) at poulpy-ckks's switching width: baby steps at the source width, giant steps at `ckks_linear_transformation_output_k(lt, src_k, dst_k)`, matrix-slot rotations and sums at `min(res.k, a.k)`, the tensor key at the wider term width, none for Galois element 1; implementations look up only the reference's keys and widths.
- **Key-use and output-layout queries.** Every operation has a public `blas_<op>_key_uses<BE: Backend>(module, [r | k], [res], inputs.., [ctx]) -> BlasKeyUses` (sorted `(Galois element, width)` pairs without repeats or the identity, plus the tensor width or `None`) and every grid-writing one a public `blas_<op>_output_layout([k], res, inputs.., [ctx]) -> BlasLayout`, the layout the call stamps, both over the infos of the `_tmp_bytes` operands without key layouts, the key-use query also dropping what keys ignore (a ciphertext product's `res`, a preparation's hint).
- **Client-side key sets.** Every operation looking up rotation keys has a key set from the cyclotomic order alone (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 12): `BlasPlan::<name>_galois_elements(cyclotomic_order, [k | r])` with `<name>` lacking `blas_` (`matmul_galois_elements` also serving the preparations and a hot prepared product), or a layout function for a batch-dependent set (`sum_galois_elements(cyclotomic_order, infos)`), `BlasPlan::galois_elements` covering context maps, each built on the host without encoding, empty when a block does not fit, and equal to the encoded maps' Galois elements (`test_blas_context`).
- **Signed rotations reduce first.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 6, signed rotations reach Galois elements only through `ckks_galois_element` or, in key sets, `rem_euclid` against the matrix-slot or slot count before `galois_elements_from_rotations`.
- **Planning reads shapes, not data.** Planning queries (key uses, output, operand and term layouts, radix helpers) take a context, public operand or their infos (`impl BlasContextInfos`, `impl BlasPtOperandInfos`) and read only map shapes rebuilt from layouts (`context_shapes`, `operand_shapes`), which depend only on the plan, `log_d`, slots, grid shape and count, never on public values (every structural diagonal stored, zero or not), and equal the encoded and prepared shapes (`test_blas_queries_from_layouts`).
- **One predicate per path.** A metadata-chosen path is decided by one shared predicate in validator, implementation, every query (key uses, output layout, scratch, radix) and tests: `replicated_at` (replicated right factor), `left_takes_replicated` (replicated-input left maps), `BlasPtAlgorithm::masked` (masked paths), `shift_maps` (shift branches).

## 5. Degree, ring and metadata

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 6 and 9.

- **The module degree only.** Under `docs/review/workspace.md` section 6 (module-degree crates), blocks, contexts and public or prepared operands are at the module degree and slot count: allocations take no degree (`blas_ciphertext_alloc(log_d, rows, cols, base2k, k)`, `_from_infos` forms at the infos' layout), validators reject others (`ensure_layouts`, `ensure_maps`) and context and operand allocations panic on them.
- **Ring-generic slot arithmetic.** Slot and batch arithmetic uses `slots_of::<R>`, `grid_batch::<BE::Ring>`, `replicated::<BE::Ring>`, `batch_fits::<BE::Ring>` or the context's or operand's own slot count, never `n / 2`, so every operation runs on both rings.
- **The batch record.** Every operation checks each input's `mat_slots` against its label `s <= log2 n` (a power of two between `(S >> s) / d^2` and `S / d^2`) and reads batches with `grid_batch::<BE::Ring>`, and in library code only implementations stamp one (besides `BlasCiphertext::from_blocks`, the label's batch, and `read_from`, the stream's): the larger operand batch for a ciphertext product, broadcasting the smaller, the source's for a preparation (0 when allocated from infos, which the prepared product rejects), the input's otherwise.
- **Untagged blocks, contract flags.** Applying `docs/review/workspace.md` section 9, written blocks have `noise = None` (`set_noise(None)` after a `ckks_copy` branch, poulpy-ckks's rotations and maps clearing it themselves) and their family's canonical flag: canonical after maps and products, flag-clear after a final lazy addition (`blas_sum`, a shift's half-map sum), `ckks_copy`'s flag after a copy.
- **Labels follow the source.** Map evaluations keep the source block's `log_sparsity` (`res.set_log_sparsity(a.log_sparsity())` after `ckks_eval_linear_transformation_into`, `mapped_block_into` in queries), products by public matrices stamp `pt_label(slots, a, count) = min(a.log_sparsity, log2(S / (count d^2)))`, ciphertext products the smaller term label.
- **`res` of any width.** Operations accept `res` of any width at the module degree and the inputs' radix and rank, only poulpy-ckks's budget check rejecting one narrower than the scale, write `k = min(res.k, natural width)`, and the key-width and parity suites cover `res` narrower and wider than the result, the value tests at and above it.
- **Queries share the stamping helpers.** Output-layout queries use the implementation's stamping helpers (`mapped_block_into`, `mapped_block`, `pt_label`, `left_term_layout`, `right_term_layout`), and a metadata change updates `# Metadata`, the query and the `test_blas_result_meta` expectations (`copied_layout`, `mapped_layout`, `product_layout`) together.
- **Map widths from poulpy-ckks.** Every width or layout a map leaves (key widths, term and staged-block layouts, output layouts, radix counts) comes from `ckks_linear_transformation_output_k` or `ckks_linear_transformation_output_layout`, never a local `k - p`, and query-less poulpy-ckks stamps (a copy, the ct x ct rule) have one tested mirror each (`copied_block`, `product_block`).

## 6. Algorithm paths

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` section 2 (every algorithm path stays exposed).

- **Two algorithm families.** `BlasPtAlgorithm` holds `Diagonal` (the default), `Jkls`, `JklsStored` and `JklsGalois`, each selected through the plan; a new path is a `Diagonal*` or `Jkls*` variant (two families only) with its own reference, key sets, output layout, radix count and tests, leaving existing circuits unchanged, while a metadata-chosen specialization is a shared predicate (section 4) between bit-identical paths or over inputs the other path cannot run (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2), not a plan variant.
- **Ciphertext products are JKLS.** `blas_matmul` and `blas_matmul_prepared` compute the JKLS circuit (sigma and tau, the phi^k and psi^k terms, one kernel call per output block, each relinearized once) with no algorithm selector.
- **New paths touch every reader.** A new path updates in one diff its variant doc (scales, storage), the plan's key sets (`matmul_pt_*_galois_elements`; `galois_elements`, `family_count` for its context maps, encoded only when the plan selects it), operand allocation and encoding (`layouts_of`, `first_map`, `block_map`, `terms_per_block`), validator, key enumerator, output-layout query, radix helper, reference, method docs' **Fixed** lists, API docs, the `blas-contracts.md` Keys table and `PT_ALGORITHMS` or `MASKED_PT_ALGORITHMS`.
- **Schedules come from the plan.** Every reader takes map schedules from `BlasPlan::strategy`: `Direct` for shift maps, their halves and replicated forms included, `optimal_bsgs_giant_step` for sigma, tau, the transpose and the public-product maps, tau's giant step for `tau_rep`, `left_mul`'s for `left_mul_rep`.
- **Context maps declared once.** A new context map adds a `MapId` with its constant, a `context_map` builder, `SINGLES` and `FAMILIES` counts (`family_count` when only some plans encode it), a `BlasContext` accessor and a `BlasPlan::galois_elements` entry (`test_blas_context`), every walk over maps following `MapId::all` through `ContextMaps::build`.
- **Hints never change bits.** Under the product's context, every mix of hot and cold `PrepareHint` operands gives the bits of `blas_matmul` on their sources, and the functional and parity suites run every `MIXES` entry.
- **Transform-domain order is fixed.** Implementations add transform-domain terms in the reference order (JKLS pairs `(a_il[k], b_lj[k])` by ascending `l` then `k`, linear-transformation sums by ascending inner index, masked products `l`-major in the operand's term order), regrouping only where the method docs list it free (the two-pass circuit, NTT backends).

## 7. Lazy sums, radix and normalization

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1 (lazy by default; headroom follows the average-case model).

- **Flag-clear inputs, lazy outputs.** Operations accept flag-clear inputs without requiring or forcing normalization (a hook normalizing a DFT-read source being a listed freedom), leave lazy outputs flag-clear, documented as `canonical = false` (section 5), and the suites feed lazy inputs (`lazy_real`, `test_blas_matmul_flag_clear`).
- **Sums normalize at the bound.** A blas sum (batch sum, rotation tree, row or column reduction) counts each input block and each canonical rotation or projection output as one lazy term and normalizes its accumulator in place before the term that would exceed the `docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1 bound, before that term's rotation when it comes from one.
- **Products cap the radix.** A ciphertext product rejects a `res` radix above 62 (`MAX_BASE2K`), and no product makes another radix-size rejection.
- **Radix helpers count every sum.** Every product path has a module-free `blas_<op>_max_base2k::<BE: MaxBase2k>(inputs.., ctx, failure_bits) -> usize` in `layouts/planning.rs` returning the largest `base2k` in `1..=62` (else 0) at which `Module::max_base2k` holds each transform-domain lazy sum (`2 L K d` JKLS limb products, `K g min(L, P)` per giant step), limbs counted at each candidate radix, kept in step with the API doc count and the `test_blas_max_base2k` hand counts.

## 8. Scratch, heap and concurrency

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 4 (scratch shortfall panics) and 5.

- **Queries beside their operation.** Each `_tmp_bytes` query sits right before its operation in the same API and OEP trait and takes, in section 2 order, grid and operand infos (`BlasGridInfos`, `BlasMatMulOperandInfos`; for the prepared product also the `blas_matmul_{left,right}_operand_layout` layouts before preparing), the context and public operand objects (reading their map layouts), key layouts (`GGLWEInfos`) and no rotation or shift amount, covering every amount (the `docs/review/workspace.md` section 5 exceptions), and panics only on layouts its validator rejects.
- **Largest step, long-lived temporaries.** A query is the `max` over its steps of each step's carve plus its largest nested query, at the blocks' live layouts, and, as the heap exception to `docs/review/workspace.md` section 5, temporaries outliving a hook call or spanning product units (preparation copies, unconverted terms, staged blocks, a shift's grid, baby-step caches, units) come from the module's factories.
- **Scratch asserted at entry.** After its validator, a reference calls `assert_scratch(op, bytes, scratch)` with its query on the layouts of the keys it looks up (`KeyLayouts`), product queries sizing the tensor key at the product width (`dnum_for_input`). A body whose first effect is a nested call that asserts, nothing carved before it, need not (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 4), and an override panics at entry with a message containing `scratch`.
- **Provided queries compose.** A provided query is the `max` of the selected `BE::` queries it calls on their layouts (`blas_rotate_rows_tmp_bytes_derived`: the map hook's queries on the maps it evaluates).
- **Streamed units, bounded heap.** A term-making product body (`blas_matmul`, a hot side of `blas_matmul_prepared`, a masked product by public matrices) makes and converts each term once, of two hot sides holds the one with fewer limbs (`a` when `(rows - 1) La <= (cols - 1) Lb` for `res` of `rows x cols` blocks, else `b` after `a`'s first unit) and streams the other in freshly allocated units, the heap staying at the held terms plus one unit (`poulpy-cpu-portable/tests/blas_streaming_memory.rs`). Term-free storage (baby-step caches, `Jkls` staged blocks) is allocated once per call and refilled per unit.
- **Per-block argument buffers.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 2, sum and kernel operands (`srcs, babies, lts`; `lefts, pts, galois`; `lhs, rhs`) are parallel slices in vectors allocated once per call and refilled per output block.
- **One level of concurrency.** blas spawns no thread, keeps no static or cross-call state (interior mutability call-local only) and runs blocks serially unless an override with serial kernels runs them concurrently, and shared types stay `Sync` (contexts, public operands, prepared terms) or `Send + Sync` (grids, prepared operands), each listed in `assert_thread_safe` (`layouts/ciphertext.rs`).

## 9. Layouts, operands and serialization

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 7 (prepared-object lifecycle), 11 and 12 (lifecycle names).

- **Layout, infos, factory.** A new blas object has a plain-data layout, an infos trait (one required method returning it, provided accessors, blanket impls for `&T` and `&mut T`) and a factory trait blanket-implemented for `Module<BE>` pairing each fill with a `_tmp_bytes` query and panicking with a static owner-naming message on a layout the module cannot hold (`assert_maps_fit`).
- **Filled in place.** `*_encode`, `blas_context_prepare`, `blas_pt_operand_prepare` and `blas_matmul_prepare_{left,right}` overwrite every allocated byte without reallocating, a refill equalling a fresh build (`test_blas_refill`), and refuse another layout before any write, factory preparations by panicking, product preparations with `InvalidOperand`.
- **Prepared product operands.** Prepared product operands are `BlasMatMulOperand<BE, P>`, allocated only by `BlasMatMulOperandPreparedFactory` from `blas_matmul_{left,right}_operand_layout` infos, filled only through `blocks_mut` (hot: a copy of the source's live limbs) or `terms_mut` (cold: `CKKSPreparedLeft`, `CKKSPreparedRight`) and recorded with `set_prepared`, which accepts only infos of the operand's shape.
- **Only `BlasCiphertext` serializes.** `BlasCiphertext` alone implements `WriterTo` and `ReaderFrom` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 11), with a little-endian header (`log_d`, rows, cols, block `n`, `base2k`, `k`, rank) and no magic or version bytes, `write_to` requiring one shared block layout and canonical blocks, `read_from` matching the full header against the receiver before any block and committing the batch record last.

## 10. Documentation and visibility

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 (minimal visibility), 13 and 14.

- **Operation docs.** An API operation's doc states what `res` receives and its circuit, notation defined once on the trait (`poulpy-blas/src/api/inter.rs`), then:
  - `# Metadata`: the poulpy-ckks operations per block and a `text` table of `k`, `log_delta`, `log_sparsity`, `slots`, `canonical`, `noise` and `mat_slots`, a preparation omitting `canonical` and `noise`;
  - `# Errors`, three bullets: the linked validator's errors in order, with `res`, its batch record and the inputs untouched; poulpy-ckks's budget errors and when they arise; "Any other `Err` is a backend fault (a validator gap or a device failure)", plus ", and `res` is then unspecified" for a grid result.
- **Query and validator docs.** A `_tmp_bytes` query has one sentence naming its operation and sized layouts ("Scratch bytes [`Self::blas_rotate`] needs into `res` from `a` with rotation keys of layout `keys`.") and, like a panicking layout query, a `# Panics` naming its validator ("As [`Self::other_tmp_bytes`], on layouts [`validate_x`] rejects." in a sibling); a validator lists its rejections in firing order under `# Errors`.
- **The contracts doc.** A new operation, path or family updates its `poulpy-blas/docs/blas-contracts.md` rows (Families and Keys tables, key-use and output-layout query lists, registration descriptions) and its `poulpy-blas/README.md` descriptions.
- **Public free functions.** The `pub` free functions are only the validators, planning queries, `grid_batch`, `replicated`, `sum_galois_elements`, `split_into_blocks`, `join_blocks` and the map builders of `layouts::maps`.

## 11. Tests and registration

Generic rules: `docs/review/workspace.md` section 15; backend registrations, module names and emulated skips: `docs/review/backends.md`.

- **The registration macros.** Every `pub fn test_blas_*` in `poulpy-blas/src/test_suite` is registered in exactly one macro: functional tests in `blas_backend_test_suite!` (an own-ring test such as `matmul_long_sums` by hand, skipping with a message when the registration sets `log_d`), parity family functions and the concurrency test in `blas_parity_test_suite!` (`inter`, `intra`, `matmul` and `concurrency` together), the conjugate-invariant test in `blas_conjugate_invariant_test_suite!` and the radix test in `blas_max_base2k_test_suite!`.
- **Every suite covers new operations.** A new operation gets:
  - a value test on its plan's key set alone, ending in `assert_grid_decrypt` with the expected layout;
  - `assert_guard` cases in its family's guards test: each `rejected_grids` and `foreign_layouts` grid as every input and `res`, misshapen grids, missing and wrong-rank keys, a budget error naming the poulpy-ckks operation;
  - cases in `test_blas_key_widths` (products also `test_blas_key_stores_from_queries`), `test_blas_context` (new key sets), `test_blas_queries_from_layouts` (new maps), `test_blas_result_meta` and `test_blas_batch_record`;
  - calls, lazy inputs and budget failures included, in its family's parity function and `test_blas_conjugate_invariant`;
  - over a hook, its (map, JKLS) entry counts and rejected inputs in `blas_hooks_run_on_accepted_calls_alone` (`poulpy-cpu-portable/src/tests/blas_overrides.rs`).
- **Invariants in the helpers.** Applying `docs/review/workspace.md` section 15, functional tests end in `assert_grid_decrypt` and parity in `compare`, and a check every operation must pass goes into them.
- **CKKS fixtures, never copies.** Suites reuse the `poulpy_ckks::test_suite` fixtures, a missing one being added there as a public helper with its CHANGELOG line.
- **Exact budgets, one byte short.** Operations and product preparations under test, and encodings and factory preparations in parity, conjugate-invariant and context tests, run on exactly their query's bytes (`poisoned_scratch`, parity's `with_scratch`), a larger shared arena building only fixtures (keys, encryptions, a value test's contexts and public operands), and each functional test also runs its operation one byte short (`assert_short_scratch` or `assert_short_scratch_panics`), expecting a panic containing `scratch`.
- **Guard tests.** Refining `docs/review/workspace.md` section 15, rejection tests use `assert_guard` or `assert_guard_on`, with a case for every validator rejection, and panic tests pin their message with `test_suite::helpers::assert_panics`.
- **Parity checks keys and inputs.** Parity cases follow the `output`, `failure` and `compare` pattern on a recording key store holding only the plan set, requiring each call's tested lookups to be a subset of the reference's, bit-identical outputs (batch record, block digits and metadata) and errors, prepared operands compared only through infos, and unchanged inputs.
- **Parity stays opaque.** Stricter than `docs/review/workspace.md` section 15, parity and concurrency tests bound both sides only by `BlasParityBackend` (`Backend<ZnxWord = i64> + BlasImpl`) and `BlasParityModule` and read results through poulpy-ckks parity snapshots, coefficients being read only in the functional and conjugate-invariant suites.
- **Skips are announced.** A case the registration cannot hold returns early after an `eprintln!` naming the test, the case and the bound it needs, and a parity test fails when `BlasParityShapes::log_d` leaves no `log_d`.
- **Host math in the clear.** Host-side math (map builders, grid helpers, label, batch and key-set arithmetic, layout queries) has `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` beside it on `Module::<HostBytesBackend>` and cleartext diagonals, covering each new map's in-the-clear identity.
- **Exact, independent fixtures.** Cleartext models use dyadic entries in `[-1, 1]` (`Grid::model`) or small integers, and long-sum tests sum distinct encryptions (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 10).

## 12. Do not report

In addition to `docs/review/workspace.md` section 18:

- Missing circuit privacy: re-randomizing or flooding `res` is the caller's job.
- A `MissingAutomorphismKey` ahead of a budget error, or a validator or hot preparation checking no CKKS budget (section 3).
- The source copy a hot (`OneShot`) preparation holds.
- Replacing the JKLS circuit of `blas_matmul` or `blas_matmul_prepared` with another algorithm.
- Bit differences between two `BlasPtAlgorithm` paths.
