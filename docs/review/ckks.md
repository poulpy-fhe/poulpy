# poulpy-ckks review rules

Read with `docs/review/REVIEW.md`; this file adds the rules specific to `poulpy-ckks`.

## 1. Layering, OEP families and macros

- **Every operation is OEP.** Every CKKS operation, fixed compositions included (affine and multiply-accumulate forms, sums, conjugate-invariant ring maps, PaCo and SHIP drivers), dispatches through an OEP method, a composition of selected operations being its derived default, and only the exemptions of `docs/review/workspace.md` section 2 have none (host adapters such as `CKKSEncodingHostOps`, aggregate queries such as `CKKSAllOpsTmpBytes`, slot counts and Galois elements from module parameters, allocation and sizing).
- **Register a new family everywhere.** A new OEP family joins in the same diff:
  - `impl_cpu_ckks_defaults!` (`poulpy-cpu-portable/src/backend_defaults.rs`) and `impl_oracle_ckks!` (`poulpy-cpu-oracle/src/ckks.rs`) when it serves both rings and no backend specializes it (else per type, `docs/review/backends.md` section 3.3);
  - when standard-only, every standard backend type's standard lists, Rayon twins included (`docs/review/backends.md` section 3.3), and `impl_oracle_ckks_standard!`, never `impl_cpu_ckks_defaults!`;
  - for a scalar-independent evaluation family, the supertraits of `CKKSImpl` (`poulpy-ckks/src/oep/ckks_impl.rs`) and its blanket impl's `where` clause, which never take a scalar-generic, fold, bootstrapping or ModUp seam, `CKKSImpl` staying the crate's only blanket `*Impl` impl;
  - the test-only fixture backends whose tests use it;
  - the family table of `poulpy-ckks/docs/ckks-contracts.md`.
- **Reference family shape.** A family's reference is `pub trait CKKS<Family>Reference<BE: Backend>` of public default-bodied `<api name>_reference` and `<api query>_reference` methods with their own `where Self:` bounds, kept off the OEP trait, and an empty `Module<BE>` impl.
- **Opt-in macro shape.** `impl_ckks_<family>_reference_full!($be:ty)` implements `$crate::oep::CKKS<Family>Impl` by forwarding every required method, and only those, reaches `anyhow` through `$crate::__private`, is re-exported by its family file and `poulpy-ckks/src/oep/mod.rs` and joins the umbrella `impl_ckks_reference_full!` (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 12.1 and 14).
- **Supertraits follow the default bodies.** A CKKS family that a provided OEP method calls through the module API is a supertrait of that OEP trait, one only a reference body composes a `where` bound of its `*_reference` method.
- **Derived bodies.** A same-layer composition is a provided OEP method calling one `pub(crate) fn <op>_derived` of `poulpy-ckks/src/oep/derived/<family>.rs` (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 3 and 14).
- **What may use `reference`.** Outside the opt-in macros, `api`, `delegates` and `oep` use only pure non-body helpers of `reference` (`ckks_one_pt`, Galois arithmetic) and core's `DiagonalProd` hook, and `layouts` may call its host plan-material generators and encoders when compiling a context (DFT and PaCo factor generators, `ckks_encode_linear_transformation_from_diagonals`) (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 3).
- **Scalar-generic seams.** Anything generic over the encoding scalar `F` is its own OEP trait (`CKKSEncodingImpl<F>`, `DFTMatrixImpl<F>`) or a method bounded by `Self: CKKSEncodingImpl<F>` (PaCo and SHIP coefficient encodings), never a parameter of `CKKSImpl` or a scalar-independent family; its API trait takes `F` as a trait parameter, its delegate impl, not its methods, requires the seam, and its reference macro covers every `F` of the backend's `CKKSEncodingImpl<F>`.
- **Core through its public seams.** A CKKS reference reaches core through its bounded core API traits and queries, pure sizing helpers, API-less helpers (`GGLWEProductReference`) and the `DiagonalProd` hook with `glwe_accumulate_streamed_baby_steps_dft`, a missing core seam being added in core, and pipeline stages through the CKKS API or stage OEP seams, never a `*_reference` body (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 3).
- **Containers hold data, modules compute.** Computations are `Module<BE>` traits, and methods of `CKKSCiphertext`, `CKKSPlaintext` and their views only manage data (`set_meta_checked`, `to_ref`) or are host-named codecs.

## 2. Naming, signatures and argument order

- **Operation grammar.** A new operation is `ckks_<op>[_<qualifier>](res, ..)`, its in-place form `_assign`, and its OEP method, API-less seams included, shares its name (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 12).
- **Operand-kind qualifiers.** The qualifier names the second operand: `_pt_vec` a plaintext polynomial, `_pt_const` one plaintext coefficient, `_ct` a ciphertext beside plaintext forms, `_prepared` a prepared operand, `_host` a host adapter; a constant is never a "cleartext".
- **Operand names.** A new operation names its destination `res`, source `a`, binary operands `a` and `b`, plaintext `pt` (indices `pt_coeff`, `res_coeff`), term slices `a` and `b` or `srcs`, `babies` and `lts`, keys `keys`, tensor key `tsk` and context `ctx`.
- **Argument order.** After `&self` come destinations, sources, per-call scalars (`k`, `bits`, indices, Galois element), caches, plans, contexts and matrices (`babies` before `lt`), keys, workers, then `scratch`, `pt_const` operations excepted (`ckks_add_pt_const(res, a, res_coeff, pt, pt_coeff, scratch)`, products without `res_coeff`), and an out-of-place form is its `_assign` form with sources inserted after `res`.
- **Encryption and setup order.** Encryption is `ckks_encrypt_sk(ct, pt, sk, source_xe, source_xa, scratch)`, decryption `ckks_decrypt(pt, ct, sk, scratch)`, and setup takes its module first after `self`, its sources after the secrets and layouts, and `scratch` last (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 10).
- **Operand bounds.** A new operation bounds its result by `GLWEToBackendMut<BE> + CKKSCtBounds + SetCKKSInfos`, ciphertext sources by `GLWEToBackendRef<BE> + CKKSCtBounds`, plaintexts also by `IntPolyInfos`, keys by `GetTensorKey<BE>` or `GetAutomorphismKey<BE>` and query key infos by `GGLWEInfos`, never a concrete `CKKSCiphertext`.

## 3. Errors and validation order

- **Typed variants for caller misuse.** A caller-fixable condition returns a `CKKSCompositionError` through the crate helpers, a recurring new one getting a helper or variant, never `ckks_ensure!` or `ckks_bail!` (kept for internal invariants, backend failures and arena checks) or a formatted `Internal` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 4):
  - `checked_log_budget_sub` (`InsufficientHomomorphicCapacity`), `checked_mul_ct_log_budget` and `checked_mul_pt_log_budget` (`MultiplicationPrecisionUnderflow`) for the budget;
  - `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_*`, `ensure_encryption_degrees`, `check_prepared_layout` (`PreparedOperandLayoutMismatch`), `set_meta_checked` (`LimbReallocationShrinksBelowMetadata`) and `CoeffsMeta::ensure_width` for operands;
  - `MissingAutomorphismKey` or `MissingRelinearizationKey`, with the lookup width and the source's `CoreError`, for keys;
  - otherwise `InvalidOperand`, `InvalidPlan` only for a plan literal, `DftMatrixMismatch` for a wrong matrix.
- **Adding a variant.** `CKKSError` and `CKKSCompositionError` stay `#[non_exhaustive]`, and a new variant serves a condition callers branch on that `InvalidOperand` cannot, carries `op` (or `plan`) and the explaining numbers, opens its `Display` with `{op}` and returns a wrapped error from `Error::source`.
- **The op label.** The `op` of an error or helper, which also starts a `ckks_ensure!` message, is the invoked `ckks_` public method, or its stem without `_assign` when one body serves both, in one `const OP` when repeated.
- **Setup errors keep the chain.** Fallible CKKS API-trait methods, queries and host adapters included, return `CKKSResult`, and setup returns `anyhow::Result` holding typed errors (`InvalidPlan { plan: "<TypeName>", .. }.into()`), adding context with `.context`, `.with_context` or `?`, never by formatting the error.
- **What may panic.** A fallible operation returns errors for caller input, panicking only in a nested scratch check (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 4), while allocation-class functions and layout queries (`*_alloc*`, `*_bytes_of*`, `take_*_scratch`, infallible `*_tmp_bytes`, `*_layout`, `*_output_k`, Galois element lists) panic with a documented `"<fn>: <requirement>"` message.
- **Validate, stamp, then work.** A fallible body checks every term, lane, output and worker (slice lengths, equal and non-zero, then layouts, scales and slot kinds, keys, budget, worker scratch), then stamps (section 6), then computes, `res` staying untouched on `Err` outside a documented partial-write path (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 4).
- **Errors in the docs.** A new or changed fallible API method lists under `# Errors` each variant it returns and when (or "As [`Self::other`]."), and states that `res` is untouched on `Err` or, for a composition failing after a write (the eager `ckks_dot_product_ct`), that it is not (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 13).
- **Plans validate once.** A setup type with invariants keeps private or `pub(crate)` fields behind constructors and builders (`new`, `from_*`, `with_*`, `compile_*`) that validate before allocating, `with_*` resetting the memoized values it affects, a struct-literal plan validates once at its compile entry (`EvalModPlan::validate`), invalid shapes are made unrepresentable (paired steps, not parallel vectors), derived plan queries stay infallible and evaluators re-check only plan-versus-runtime clauses.
- **Plan literals carry `CoeffsMeta`.** A plan literal carries `CoeffsMeta`, never a layout, and every encoder allocating from one calls `CoeffsMeta::ensure_width(op)` first.

## 4. Scratch, allocation and prepared operands

- **Temporaries come from scratch.** A leveled operation carves its temporary ciphertexts and plaintexts in `scratch.scope(..)` with `ScratchArenaTakeCKKS`, the heap holding only caller-kept objects and commented per-call outputs whose count the plan sets or that cross threads (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 5).
- **Allocation goes through `CKKSModuleAlloc`.** Ciphertexts, plaintexts and linear transformations come from default methods of `CKKSModuleAlloc` (empty `Module<BE>` impl), size first (none for a ciphertext, at the module degree), then `base2k`, `k`, `rank`, `_from_infos` copying the CKKS metadata and `_from_glwe_infos` dropping it (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 6 and 14).
- **Prepared-operand lifecycle.** A CKKS prepared type is allocated through its OEP family by `*_alloc_from_infos(infos, hint)`, sized by `*_bytes_of_from_infos(infos, hint)` and filled by `ckks_prepare_<side>(res, a, scratch)`, which refuses another degree, radix, width or rank with `InvalidOperand`; its infos carry the source's layout and metadata but no noise, its `PrepareHint` sets only the representation, and products check it against `res` with `check_prepared_layout` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 7).
- **Caller-owned modules, workers and outputs.** Host-side setup takes the caller's `&Module<HostBytesBackend<R>>` and transfers explicitly, pipeline outputs are caller-allocated, and parallel PaCo checks every `PaCoWorker`'s degree and scratch against its own module before any branch, recombining in increasing order (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2).
- **One query per operation stem.** A scratch-taking operation has one `<op stem>_tmp_bytes` shared by its out-of-place and `_assign` forms, `_assign` callers passing `res` as result and operand, or the family query its doc names (`ckks_prepare_tmp_bytes` for `ckks_prepare_left` and `ckks_prepare_right`, `ckks_dft_tmp_bytes` for DFT format wrappers), and no query outside the limb-wise families copies their `res_size: usize` form (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 5 and 12).
- **An `_assign` copy is counted.** An `_assign` needing the old `res` copies it with `glwe_copy` into a scoped temporary and runs the out-of-place form, its query adding `BE::scratch_aligned(glwe_bytes_of_from_infos(copy))` to the maximum of `glwe_copy_tmp_bytes` and the out-of-place query, and one needing no copy reserves none.
- **Queries cover every route.** A query maximizes over every runtime route (one-pass or tensor, prepared or streamed diagonals, lazy or eager sums, every rotation-key layout, the work layout and the caller's destination), sizing a nested call on the caller's destination at its allocation (`max_k`) and a temporary at its carve width (`res.k()`) (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 5).
- **Concurrent stages split the arena.** A reference running stages through `BE::TaskExecutor::join` splits the arena under the same `TaskExecutor::IS_PARALLEL` predicate as its query's stage count times `worker_scratch_bytes::<BE>(stage query)` reservation, its serial branch running the same stages in order (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 5).
- **Staged compilation.** A compiled context whose preparation budget depends on generated material offers `compile_unprepared`, `prepare_tmp_bytes` from the selected queries and a freeing `prepare`, beside a one-shot `compile` documented to cover both budgets.
- **Pipeline queries validate.** PaCo and SHIP `_tmp_bytes` queries return `CKKSResult<usize>` after runtime validation, and their drivers check the arena against them before any write.

## 5. Ring, slots and keys

- **Standard-only families by type.** A family or composition needing complex slots or standard-ring automorphisms (conjugation, multiplication by `i`, homomorphic DFT, EvalMod, bootstrapping and ModUp, fold, PaCo and SHIP coefficient encodings, the standard side of the ring maps) bounds `Backend<Ring = Standard>` on its trait or impl, directly or through a supertrait (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 6).
- **The ring is a parameter.** CKKS containers carry their ring as a type parameter, implementing backend conversions only for `R = BE::Ring`, and ring-generic signatures name it (`CKKSFoldOps<BE, R>`).
- **Slots and Galois elements.** Ring-shared code takes slot counts from `ckks_max_slots` and `ckks_slots_of_degree` and rotations from `ckks_galois_element(k)`, conjugating with `-1` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 6).
- **Identity rotations are keyless.** A rotation by Galois element 1, baby and giant step 0 included, is a copy or direct fold that looks up no key and records no noise, and key sets omit element 1.
- **Key widths at lookup.** Keys are looked up, sized and pre-checked at the width the switch decomposes: `min(a.k(), res.k())` for an out-of-place rotation or conjugation, `res.k()` for `_assign`, the widest operand (`res` included for `_assign`) for a tensor product, `a.k()` for baby steps, the output width for giant steps, the branch width in PaCo, the width model's stage width in bootstrapping (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 4).
- **Key sets from the schedule.** A key-consuming pipeline derives its `galois_elements` query from the schedule it evaluates (or a replay sharing it), key generation, validation and docs using it instead of a hand-kept list, and takes keys through caller-implemented sources (`GetAutomorphismKey`, `GetTensorKey`, `BootstrappingKeys`, `PaCoKeys`) (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2).
- **Row-offset keys.** A switch that cannot honour a row offset refuses a `k_offset > 0` key before use, and one using it checks `check_offset_raise` before any work.
- **Shared key validators.** Pipelines validate gadget keys, reported key metadata and storage capacity before any work with the `poulpy-ckks/src/layouts/validation.rs` helpers, checking both the reported infos and the backend view (`validate_gadget_key` and `validate_gadget_backend_view`, `validate_storage_capacity` and `validate_backend_storage_capacity`).

## 6. Metadata and stamping

- **The observable fields.** Every operation, overrides included, stamps all output metadata itself (`k`, base2k, rank, scale, `SlotsKind`, `log_sparsity`), `log_budget` being derived as `k - log_delta` and no CKKS type storing a budget or its own metadata struct (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 9).
- **No rescale operation.** No rescale operation exists or is added: products rescale through `cnv_offset` (to `min(log_delta)`, or the ciphertext's against a plaintext), chained transforms carry none between factors, results land at `res.k()`, never at the limb-aligned allocation, and only `ckks_div_pow2_into` and `ckks_div_pow2_assign` move budget bits under `log_delta`.
- **Stamp before the rounding write.** Offsets are computed against the pre-stamp `res` and the final metadata stamped before any step rounding into `res`, except:
  - an `_assign` product reading `res` stamps after its apply step and before relinearization (`StampOrder::AfterApply`) or works from a copy;
  - a one-pass product taking the output width as an argument stamps right after the call;
  - lazy flag-clearing outputs, `set_slots` and `set_log_sparsity` may follow the write.
- **One shared formula per rule.** Offsets, budgets and `cnv_offset` come only from the shared helpers: `ckks_shift_stamp_unary` or `ckks_copy_stamp_unary` for unary shift-and-relabel, `ckks_offset_binary` (`min(log_delta) + min(log_budget) - res.k()`, saturating) for add and sub, `get_mul_ct_params` or `mul_ct_params_raw` for ciphertext products, and `get_mul_pt_params` or `mul_pt_params_raw` for every plaintext product (diagonals, fused BSGS combinations, SHIP masks); a fused alignment repeats `ckks_offset_unary`, `checked_log_budget_sub`, `set_meta(a.meta())`, `set_log_budget`, then the kernel, on the direct path when `ckks_unary_exact` holds.
- **Relabel helpers by accounting.** Copying a source's labels is `set_meta(a.meta())`, `set_log_budget(..)`, then `set_log_delta` if the scale moves, a lift spending budget at a fixed modulus changes the scale through the meta, never `set_log_delta`, and after a stamp lowers `k` the canonical flag is restored only on a value canonical at the new width (section 7).
- **Slot kinds.** Every operation stamps its `SlotsKind` (`Integer < Real < Complex`) by these rules, never finer, refusing `Integer` input if it can break integrality (`div_pow2`):
  - the operands' `join` for sums, products, dot products and linear transformations, diagonals included;
  - `Complex` after multiplying or dividing by `i` or adding a constant at a nonzero coefficient;
  - `Real` or a `meet` only where an identity proves it (`z + conj(z)`, traces, conjugate-invariant ring maps, real-valued polynomials, EvalMod, LUT and bootstrap outputs, the slot transform's real and imaginary halves), encoding using `meet(BE::Ring::SLOTS)`.
- **Sparsity composition.** `log_sparsity` is the operands' minimum (`log2(N / n)` for a degree-`n` plaintext, `lt_log_sparsity` for a diagonal), and an operation moving coefficient positions sets it explicitly: repack moves it by one, merging distinct inputs clears it, a PaCo branch stamps its gap.
- **Noise metadata.** An operation relabelling or forwarding without writing data (`div_pow2_assign`, identity rotation, one-term `add_many`, the DFT chain) clears the noise estimate, only a zero-offset copy keeps it, re-ranked to the destination, and encryption records `ComponentNoise::from_secret_at` last (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 9).
- **Encryption stamps, decryption does not.** Encryption stamps the plaintext's `log_delta`, slot kind and `log2(N / n)` sparsity with `log_budget = k - log_delta`, normalizing once, and `ckks_decrypt` and `ckks_extract_pt` write coefficients only, at the preset alignment (`PlaintextAlignmentImpossible`), into degree `n < N` only when `log2(N / n) <= ct.log_sparsity()` (`PlaintextDegreeMismatch`).
- **Copy CKKS values with `ckks_copy`.** A CKKS result moves into a CKKS destination with `ckks_copy`, `glwe_copy` only into one already carrying the intended label or relabelled right after.
- **Output queries match the stamp.** A public output query (`ckks_linear_transformation_output_k`, `ckks_fold_layout`) applies its evaluation's stamping rule and changes with it, the reference deriving its output from it or debug-asserting agreement.
- **Document the metadata rule.** A stamping API trait documents under `# Metadata` its `offset`, `log_delta_out` and `log_budget_out` formulas per differing variant family in a `text` block, plus slot kind, sparsity, flag effect and preconditions with their errors, on the trait when shared (methods saying "See the trait-level documentation") (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 13).

## 7. Laziness and normalization

- **Lazy linear operations.** Additions and subtractions, plaintext forms included, and `ckks_double_into` write raw limb sums and clear the flag after validation; negation, exact copies, multiplication or division by `i` and `div_pow2_assign` keep it; products, rotations, conjugation, key switches and decryption output canonical values; linear operations normalize only through an aligning shift (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1).
- **Raw limb shortcuts.** A limb-wise operation skips the normalizing `glwe_lsh` shift only when `ckks_unary_exact` or `ckks_holds_limbs` holds (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8).
- **Manual canonical flags.** A poulpy-ckks body sets the flag only after a normalization of every column, as `set_canonical(a.is_canonical())` on exactly copied or permuted digits, or, commented, after a stamp lowered `k` on a value canonical at the new width (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8).
- **Shared values normalized once.** Power-basis entries and the lazy tensor sum before relinearization are normalized once where produced, a per-term sum normalization commenting its bound (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1).
- **Lazy sums.** A long CKKS sum normalizes in place at the shared average-case bound or a caller-set cadence, never rejecting a term count or radix for its length, and summed tensor products keep `base2k <= 62`, past which the ciphertext dot product goes eager and the prepared one returns `InvalidOperand` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1).

## 8. Products and DFT economy

- **Accumulate in the transform domain.** CKKS sums of products and key switches (SHIP masking and mux, the raise's first factor, BSGS baby-step combinations, dot-product relinearization) stay in DFT or BIG form until one inverse transform and normalization per column, unless a stated bound forces per-term work (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.2).
- **Prepare a reused operand once.** An operand several products read in a row (a giant-step power, SHIP `pi` plaintexts, a diagonal set) is prepared once with `PrepareHint::Reuse` and kept until its last product, counted in the query or documented as a heap-owned prepared object.
- **Hoisted DFTs share one grid.** An input shared by several key switches is transformed once only when all keys have whole-limb digits or share one `(k_digit, dnum)`, after checking it against every key's `max_input_k`.
- **Stages at their narrowest width.** A pipeline runs each stage at the width the remaining circuit needs: products and transforms at `res.k()`, the DFT chain factor by factor in place, a PaCo accumulator at `output_k + circuit_depth`.
- **Fused paths fall back.** The gated fused paths (BSGS baby-step combination, one-pass product and relinearization, lazy tensor sum) fall back to the composed per-term path (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 4).

## 9. Plaintexts and encoding

- **Plaintexts by slot count.** A message of `m` slots is allocated with `ckks_pt_vec_alloc(m, base2k, k)`, at degree `2m` on the standard ring and `m` on the conjugate-invariant ring, panicking below `MIN_DEGREE`, and plaintext operands carry the `IntPolyInfos` bound (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 12).
- **Plaintext operand checks.** Before any write, a `_pt_vec` operation runs `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_degree_embeds(op, ct_n, pt_n, BE::MIN_DEGREE)` and `ensure_plaintext_alignment`, and a `_pt_const` one, with no degree check, runs `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_coeff_in_range` for each index against its own operand's degree (`"ciphertext"`, `"plaintext"`) and `ensure_plaintext_alignment`.
- **One value per coefficient.** Encoders and decoders take exactly one value per plaintext coefficient or `ckks_slots_of_degree(n)` planar `[re | im]` slots (`2m` scalars for `m` slots), returning an error for any other count and never spreading values at a gap.
- **Quantization rejects before writing.** Every encoding path, overrides included, quantizes through `ckks_quantize` or `ckks_quantize_i64`, never an `as` cast, rejecting a non-finite value or one outside the codec range (`i64` while `log_delta + log_budget <= 63`, else `i128`) before any write, and keeps the preset metadata, narrowing only the slot kind with `meet`.
- **Resident encoding signatures.** Encoding API and OEP signatures take only backend-resident buffers (`CKKSEncodingBuffer`) and plaintext views, host slices entering only through host-named adapters (`CKKSEncodingHostOps`) staging through the caller's arena with `copy_host_into_encoding_buffer` and `copy_encoding_buffer_into_host` (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 7).
- **Encoding plans in the cache.** Encoding plans are backend-owned, cached per scalar in the module's `ModulePlanCache` (`ckks_encoding_plan_cache_impl`, built by `ckks_encoding_plans_create_impl`) once per key, never rebuilt per call or kept in a global or thread-local, and encoding operations take no plan argument.

## 10. Numerics and determinism

- **Setup math through `CKKSFloat`.** Production code calls no `f32`, `f64` or `num_traits::Float` transcendental, `sqrt`, `powi`, `powf`, `cbrt` or `hypot`: setup values are computed in the working scalar `F` through `CKKSFloat`, irrational constants never pass through `f64`, a wider intermediate narrows to `F` mantissa-exactly, and a new transcendental joins `CKKSFloat` (libm for `f32` and `f64`, portable binary128 for `Quad`) with `f32`, `f64` and `num_traits::Float` entries in `poulpy-ckks/clippy.toml` under the shared reason, none for `f128`.
- **`Quad` math by hand.** `Quad` math outside `scalar::backing` goes through `Float` or `CKKSFloat`, never the inner `f128`; only `scalar::backing` picks the backing (`CKKSFloat` always the portable one), and `libquadmath` changes no stored bit, codec or type.
- **Platform-math allows.** Outside test code (`cfg(test)` and the `test-utils` `test_suite`), `allow(clippy::disallowed_methods)` marks only a deliberate platform-math route, on the narrowest item or example root, with a same-line reason comment.
- **libquadmath stays allowlisted.** The `f128` crate appears only in the x86_64 Linux GNU target tables of `poulpy-ckks/Cargo.toml`, behind the non-default `libquadmath` feature or as a parity-test dev-dependency, the pure-Rust `astro-float-num` and `libm` backing staying on every target.
- **Byte-identical encoding.** Encoding, decoding and bootstrapping setup constants are byte-identical on every backend and platform at each precision in both rings, `CKKSEncodingImpl` overrides following the portable arithmetic (`docs/review/backends.md` section 5.9) under the determinism fixtures (section 15.4; `docs/review/workspace.md` section 1).
- **The oracle derives roots independently.** A change to `CKKSFloat::ckks_root_of_unity`, `ROOT_TABLE_LOG_ORDER` or the slot-transform butterflies or their rounding is matched in `poulpy-cpu-oracle/src/ckks_roots.rs` and `ckks_fft.rs`, neither side adjusted to the other's values, keeping `production_roots_match_independent_derivation` passing.
- **Sealed PaCo and SHIP scalars.** PaCo and SHIP encodings accept only the sealed `PaCoScalar` and `ShipScalar` (`f64`, `Quad`), never unsealed or extended to `f32`, rejecting `log_q >= MANTISSA_BITS` (PaCo) or `base2k >= MANTISSA_BITS` (SHIP).

## 11. Linear transformations and diagonals

- **Scale-agnostic engines live in core.** CKKS linear-transformation and polynomial-evaluation code holds only scale, budget and `cnv_offset` accounting, encoding and the Galois-to-key map, scale-free machinery going to poulpy-core.
- **A diagonal stores one period.** `ComplexDiagonals` entries follow `docs/review/core.md` section 8.4 (diagonal periods), storing the shortest predictable period, an absent part counting 1 in `period_lcm`, and a bit-reversed layer is replicated to the slot count first.
- **One period per transformation.** `build_transform` hands over every diagonal at the map period `ComplexDiagonals::period()`, encoded at its `period_degree` (`2 * period` standard, `period` invariant), a period whose degree is below the backend floor repeated, and callers size the encoders with `ckks_encode_linear_transformation_tmp_bytes(module, p)` at that period or a bound, never from a ciphertext layout.
- **Uniform diagonals.** Encoders, hand-built transform paths included, produce diagonals of one scale, width, slot kind and degree, and every consumer of a caller-built transformation, the prepare included, checks scale, width and slot kind in release with `ensure_uniform_diagonals` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 4).
- **Summed transforms check every term.** A summed linear transformation checks before any write the slice lengths, every term against the first (schedule, diagonal layout, width, scale, slot kind, source layout and scale), each baby cache's rotation coverage before reading its layout, and every key.
- **Every BSGS path stays selectable.** DFT factors take their giant step from the plan's schedule, `optimal_bsgs_giant_step` and `DFTPlan::with_optimal_bsgs` stay opt-in, and `Direct`, `Bsgs`, prepared or streamed diagonals, PaCo slot orders and psi tails, C2S-first, S2C-first, EvalRound+ and the raise's first factor stay caller-selectable (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2).
- **One DFT generator.** Every homomorphic DFT factor comes from `gen_dft_matrices` or `gen_dft_matrices_blockwise` under the crate's conventions (generator-5 embedding, natural slot order), scheme conventions (bit reversal, slot order, fused masks) being post-transforms of those factors (`compose`), never a private factor stack or FFT.
- **Replays share the generator's geometry.** Value-free replays (`DFTPlan::diagonal_indexes`, `galois_elements`) share the generator's `dft_layer_*` helpers, and a generator change keeps `plan_diagonal_indexes_match_generated_factors` and `plan_diagonal_log_sparsity_matches_generated_factors` passing.
- **Schedules in evaluation order.** A `DFTPlan` schedule lists factors in evaluation order for `Encode` and `Decode` alike, never reordered by kind, an inverting Decode using the reversed schedule the caller passes.
- **Direction and format are types.** A DFT matrix carries direction and format as sealed type markers, each evaluation entry takes its exact `DFTMatrix<BE, Dir, Fmt, ..>`, and only `ckks_new_dft_matrix` resolves them at runtime, validating hand-built factors and refusing a dense `RepackImagAsReal` with `DftMatrixMismatch`.

## 12. Bootstrapping, PaCo and SHIP

- **Bootstrap entry checks.** `ckks_bootstrap_reference` and `ckks_functional_bootstrap_reference` check before any work the context kind, rank 1, encapsulation keys exactly when the recipe encapsulates, and every key's coverage of the run's widths (`check_key_widths`, `check_raise_keys`), new key-consuming stages included, only the EvalRound+ bypass relying on stage lookups.
- **One key-width model.** Preset key layouts, entry checks and stage-width computations derive widths only from `BootstrappingPlan::key_widths()`, which a change of a stage's working width updates, EvalRound+ bypass keys staying outside it at the raised-width shape, and the bootstrap scratch query maximizes over every rotation-key layout at the widest ciphertexts.
- **Preset names.** A bootstrapping preset is named `n{log_n}_d{log_delta}_k{output_k}_p{log2_precision}_{c2s|s2c}` plus the technique suffixes of `poulpy-ckks/src/presets/bootstrapping.rs`, renamed when `k` or `p` changes, its unit tests named `<preset>_<property>`; a test-only preset is `insecure_`-prefixed, `pub(crate)` and gated on `test-utils`.
- **Registering a preset.** A public preset `pub fn <name>() -> anyhow::Result<BootstrappingPreset>` calls `build(PresetSpec { name: "<name>", .. })` with its measured `log2_precision` rounded down and lands in `presets::bootstrapping::all()` in stable order, `all_lists_every_preset_once`, a `<name>_is_composable_and_within_bounds` test pinning its widths, consumed bits and key shapes, and a doc comment and `docs/bootstrapping.md` row stating those widths; `bootstrapping_presets_meet_precision` checks both real and imaginary slots, and an `insecure_` preset stays out of `all()` with its own default-run test.
- **Preset key shapes are derived.** `build` derives every preset key layout from its use through `key_layout`, `widest_k_digit` and `key_fits` (whole-limb digits in the fewest rows the modulus bound admits, the `GGLWELayout::dnum_for_input` digit count, a `k_aux` guard of at least one digit plus `KEY_NOISE_MARGIN`, each key covering its stage's widest input), `with_dsizes` being the only override, under the same fit and modulus validation (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2).
- **Preset modulus bounds.** Every preset declares a cited modulus bound per secret (dense and sparse), which `validate_modulus_bounds` checks from `build`, `with_base2k` and `with_dsizes` against the bootstrap width and every key layout's gadget, auxiliary and total modulus, each under its own secret's bound.
- **PaCo slot order and keys.** `PaCoSlotOrder` defaults to `Natural` and stays stable for persisted keys, a convention change touches only its relabel sites (`paco_c2s_factors`, the packing gather, `paco_stc_factors`) behind the flag, and key generation uses `plan.galois_elements()`.
- **PaCo and SHIP hooks.** `CKKSPaCoCoeffEncodingImpl` and `CKKSShipCoeffEncodingImpl`, with no reference macro, are the only required PaCo and SHIP OEP methods, agreeing within CKKS precision with their `*_host` definitions, every other PaCo and SHIP OEP method being a derived default, and a new required PaCo or SHIP method states why existing APIs fall short.
- **Key-bundle checks are structural.** PaCo and SHIP key-bundle constructors and preflight validate structure only (layouts, metadata, Galois labels, gadget dimensions, capacity, fingerprint) (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 18).
- **A new fold ring.** A new fold input ring implements the crate-private `FoldRing<BE>` step in `poulpy-ckks/src/reference/fold.rs`, reusing the shared fold and unfold body.

## 13. Polynomial evaluation and approximation

- **Depth from core's model.** Degree and depth selection uses core's `bsgs_eval_depth` and `BSGSPolynomial::eval_depth`, never a local formula.
- **`consumed_bits` is the budget prediction.** Every compiled plan or approximation exposes `consumed_bits()` and its depth from its evaluation's accounting, the evaluation checks `log_budget >= consumed_bits()` or predicts its output metadata before any write, a predicting pipeline re-checks the evaluated width at the end, and budget changes update `consumed_bits()` and its pinned tests.
- **Shared scratch bound.** Polynomial evaluation has no query of its own, callers sizing it by `ckks_all_ops_tmp_bytes` or `ckks_all_ops_with_atk_tmp_bytes` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 5), so a new callee, larger callee query or BSGS temporary in it, its `BSGSOps` implementation, the power basis or the approximation layer updates the `CKKSAllOpsTmpBytes` maximum, and EvalMod's query when EvalMod reaches it; DFT and EvalMod queries stay outside that maximum.
- **Approximation entry points.** A host fitting or selection entry point comes as `name` (default `RemezOptions`) and `name_with(.., opts)` over one `*_multi_interval_with` body, validates every scalar argument first (finite, positive, ordered disjoint intervals, nonzero options), with family-prefixed errors, documents callbacks as pure and reads Remez samples from its per-fit cache.

## 14. Containers, secrets and serialization

- **`CKKSInfos` only on CKKS containers.** `CKKSInfos`, hence `SetCKKSInfos` and `CKKSCtBounds`, is implemented only on CKKS containers, views, prepared operands, `CKKSLayout` and crate-private scratch infos, never a core type, `SetCKKSInfos` staying blanket-implemented.
- **New scratch-backed views.** A scratch-backed CKKS container is a `view_wrapper!`-style struct, one `impl_ckks_infos!` (`ckks_inner` around a CKKS container, `glwe_inner` around a GLWE view), hand-written `GLWEToBackendRef` and `GLWEToBackendMut` and a `ScratchArenaTakeCKKS` carve, a flagless plaintext view making `set_canonical` and `set_noise` no-ops, and a container dereferencing to a core type implements `Clone` by hand, bounded on the inner `Clone` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8).
- **Setup-created secrets are encapsulated.** A secret that CKKS setup creates or derives is drawn from a caller-passed source and tagged `Distribution::ENCAPSULATED("<name>")`, an ephemeral one never leaving its call (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 10).
- **Wipe the CKKS carves.** A CKKS operation carving secret-derived temporaries itself (key generation, `ckks_decrypt`) wipes them before returning, whatever its core callees wipe (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 10).
- **Only `CKKSCiphertext` serializes.** `CKKSCiphertext` alone serializes, as its GLWE with no CKKS header, plaintext metadata included, and key bundles store core key types (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 11).
- **Module visibility.** `delegates` stays private, the `oep` family modules private behind re-exports of each `*Impl` trait and macro (derived bodies: section 1), `error` private behind root re-exports of `CKKSError`, `CKKSCompositionError` and `CKKSResult` and `pub(crate)` ones of `ckks_ensure!`, `ckks_bail!` and its helpers, and the pipeline submodules (PaCo context, keyset, plan, secret) `pub(crate)` behind re-exports of their public types; the root re-exports the core metadata types of CKKS signatures, and `reference` is `pub`, its helpers `pub` only for another crate or an override (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 2 and 14).

## 15. Tests and registration

### 15.1. Registration

- **Register every suite test.** A new functional `pub fn test_*` in `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/<module>.rs` joins the `ckks_backend_test_suite!` list of `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/mod.rs` without its `test_` prefix (a second registration with another argument under its own descriptive name), through `run_test_result!` for `anyhow::Result<()>`, `run_test_with_arg!` for a trailing argument, `run_params_test!` for modules built from `params`, `run_test_ignored!` for a paper-scale or too-slow case beside its enabled small case, else `run_test!`, or, when its signature does not fit, from the macro body, a new module being declared `pub mod <name>;` there (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Suite test signatures.** An independent-suite test is `pub fn test_<name><BE, F, E>(params: CKKSTestParams, module: &Module<BE>, host_module: &Module<HostBytesBackend>)` with `BE: TestContextBackend<Ring = Standard>` and `F: TestScalar`, and a parity helper `pub fn test_<area>_parity<BR, BT, F>(params, reference, tested)`.
- **Rank-2 coverage.** A rank-generic operation registers at least one test also in `ckks_backend_rank2_test_suite!` under the same name, reading the rank from `params`, never `Rank(1)`.
- **Conjugate-invariant tests.** Conjugate-invariant tests live in `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/conjugate_invariant.rs`, one `#[test]` per case named `ckks_ci_*` inside `conjugate_invariant_ckks_test_suite!`, each building its own modules (invariant at `params.n`, standard sibling at `2n` or `4n`).
- **Shared helpers stay ring-generic.** `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/helpers.rs` helpers bound only `BE: TestContextBackend` (or a transfer bound) and `Module<BE>: TestContextSharedModule<BE>`, taking `&Module<HostBytesBackend<BE::Ring>>`.
- **Parity helpers on every backend.** A parity helper in `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/parity/`, re-exported from its `mod.rs`, joins these `ckks_parity_test_suite!` lists of every backend crate running CKKS parity, oracle included, or, for one backend's own path, of that backend (`docs/review/backends.md` section 13.2):
  - the `_f64` and `_quad` lists;
  - the `ckks_parity_ci_*` lists unless a bound, direct or through an OEP supertrait, needs `Ring = Standard`;
  - the standard and invariant rank-2 lists if rank-generic;
  - the `f32` lists, where kept, for encoding or DFT-matrix generation at `F`, never PaCo or SHIP encoding.
- **CI tokens.** A check meant for the native AVX-512 lane (tokens `ckks_parity`, `determinism`) or the manual NEON step (`ckks_parity`), no SDE run selecting CKKS tests, is a parity helper registered under a `ckks_parity_[ci_]<backend>_<case>` module or a fixture test whose path contains `determinism` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 16).

### 15.2. Parity

- **Two suites, two jobs.** The independent suite checks cleartext expectations, the noise model and precision bounds, never comparing backends; a parity helper checks only bit-exact agreement, never a cleartext expectation or noise bound, using no secret key except encryption parity's fixed-digit secret, and a check needing opaque storage is a parity helper (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Parity drivers stay opaque.** Parity helpers and determinism drivers bound only `Backend<ZnxWord = i64>`, the traits they call and transfer bounds such as `HostStaged` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Shape of a parity helper.** A helper runs one deterministic driver per backend on `fixture_*` inputs and `parity::keys`, returning `Snapshot`s of every output, calls `assert_untagged` on the reference's evaluation outputs, then `assert_eq!(want, driver(tested))`, each driver asserting sources and failed destinations unchanged and running on its backend's own query.
- **Parity sweeps the metadata.** A driver sweeps each metadata dimension its operation reads or stamps (narrower and wider destinations, off-grid widths, zero and positive `log_sparsity`, every stamped slot kind, unequal scales, smaller-degree plaintexts, the rank of `params`) and, for an operation reading through a DFT, lazy and canonical operands (`fixture_operand(.., lazy)`), flagging raw-digit fixtures as their digits are.
- **Prepared storage is never compared.** Prepared operands, DFT matrices and encoding plans are compared across backends only through their consumers' results, diagonal plaintexts through `diagonal_snapshot`, and prepared bytes only within one backend (prepare-into against prepare) (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 6 and 15).
- **Encoding parity tolerances.** Float transforms compare within `transform_tolerance` (`F::epsilon() * 64 * n` at the scalar's own precision, never reduced to `f64`), slot-composition methods byte for byte, sign of zero included, the coefficient codec by `Snapshot`, and PaCo and SHIP embeddings by metadata and decoded coefficients within `transform_tolerance` plus two quantization steps.
- **Encryption parity.** Encryption parity registers only through `ckks_encryption_parity_test_suite!`, its driver also returning each source's post-call state (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 10).

### 15.3. Functional tests

- **Exact budget per call.** The operation under test runs in `parity::helpers::with_scratch` at its own query for the call's operands, the shared `alloc_scratch` arena serving only setup (keys, encryption, decryption) (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Decrypt through the checked helpers.** Tests decrypt through `assert_decrypt_precision`, `assert_decrypt_precision_at_log_delta` (when a lower-scale operand bounds the precision), `ckks_decrypt_with_prec`, `ckks_decrypt_decode` or a file-local helper calling `assert_canonical_at_k` first, never a bare `ckks_decrypt`; exact standard-ring operations compare against expected values from the quantized inputs (`quantize`, `quantized_const`, `quantized_vector`), never an absolute tolerance, and approximating pipelines use `precision_stats` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Floors document their measurement.** A test's precision or noise floor (`MIN_AVG_LOG2_PREC`, `NOISE_GUARD_BITS`, `PRECISION_GUARD_BITS`, a noise bound) states its measurement or model and margin, and moves only with a new measurement.
- **Output metadata from the inputs.** Tests assert output metadata against values computed from the inputs and the destination's `k` before the call (`assert_*_output_meta`, with `a.k()` and a `ckks_snapshot` taken before mutation for `_assign`, `assert_ct_meta` for composites), and leveled pipelines their exact `log_budget` use against `consumed_bits()` and the preserved `log_delta`.
- **Noise tags in op tests.** Encryption tests assert the recorded fresh estimate (`precision() == ct.k()`, parties, rank, distribution, sigma) and the error's measured second moment against the model, and copy tests pin that an equal-width `ckks_copy` keeps the estimate while a narrowing copy, a one-term `add_many` and a prepared operand carry none.
- **Rejection tests pin the variant.** A rejection test pins the `CKKSCompositionError` variant and `op` (`assert_ckks_error`, or `matches!` on `err.composition()`), asserts the destination's `snapshot` unchanged, runs pre-scratch checks on `with_scratch(0, ..)` and, for a documented order, breaks two checks and asserts the earlier; a parity driver may assert only `is_err()` with an unchanged snapshot when the independent test pins the variant (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Arithmetic case matrix.** A new leveled out-of-place and `_assign` operation has `_aligned`, `_smaller_output` (off-grid at `k - base2k - 1`) and `_assign` tests, a second operand adding `_delta_log_delta` (lower scale) and `_delta_a_lt_b`, `_delta_a_gt_b`, `_assign_self_lt`, `_assign_self_gt` (budgets a limb apart), plus one `_error` test per typed rejection.
- **Paths stay pinned.** A test named after an internal path asserts, with a message, the condition selecting it, and a runtime predicate choosing circuits is tested on both sides and at its boundary.
- **Fused forms equal their composition.** A fused, summed, prepared, one-shot, view-input or parallel form, an orchestrator and a one-term sum equal by `snapshot` (digits, layout, flag, noise tag) the composition or form their contract names (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 15).
- **Keys in tests.** Test keys cover the widest input switched (`ksk_layout(k_in)` for a wider input), and a diff touching key lookup or width selection tests an input one bit past `max_input_k` (typed error, nothing written) and pins the looked-up width with `TensorKeyAt` or `QueryLog` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 2).
- **Lazy-sum tests sum independent encryptions.** A lazy-sum headroom test sums one fresh `ckks_encrypt` per term, never copies of one ciphertext (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 8.1).
- **Transforms against independent references.** A transform is tested in each direction against a reference independent of production code (`ReferenceEncoder`, a cleartext model, `ComplexDiagonals::evaluate`), not only by a round trip, the independent suite encoding inputs and expected values on the host with `ReferenceEncoder`.
- **Prepare-into lifecycle tests.** A prepare-into (or encode-into) object is tested per side and `PrepareHint` for `bytes_of` equal to its allocation, prepare-into equal to prepare, a `0xA5`-poisoned refill matching without reallocating, and another layout refused with `InvalidOperand`, bytes and infos unchanged.
- **Seeds per call.** Test encryption and key-generation helpers (`ckks_encrypt*`, `gen_tsk`, `gen_atk`, `gen_switching_key`, `gen_ci_keys`) draw from `next_test_seed(tag)` sources, a fixed `Source::new([x; 32])` serving only secret keys, messages, parity fixtures, single draws and unconsumed-source rejection tests (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 10).
- **Test degrees and radix.** A test degree below `N` derives from `BE::MIN_DEGREE` (both backends' in parity) or a ratio clearing every floor, a case below the floor being skipped or asserted to fail, and derived parameters use struct update over `CKKSTestParams`, keeping `params.base2k` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 6).
- **Plaintext vocabulary in tests.** Tests allocate messages with `ckks_pt_vec_alloc(slots, ..)`, a decryption target allowed at `ckks_plaintext_alloc(ct.n(), ..)` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 12).
- **Host setup math tests.** Host-only setup code (factor generation, Remez, presets, plans) has a same-file `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` with exact pins, backend-needing tests and cleartext oracles living in `test_suite`.

### 15.4. Fixtures

- **Determinism fixture keys.** A fixture key names only ring, precision, degree and case (`encoding-{ring}-f{bits}-n{n}-slots{s}-..`, `setup-f{bits}-..`), never a backend, and `poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/determinism.txt` holds exactly the keys the drivers produce, a diff adding a key committing its line and one removing or renaming a key deleting the stale line (`docs/review/workspace.md` sections 15 and 16).
- **Fixture inputs are exact.** Fixture values come from platform-independent inputs (dyadic values, literals, correctly rounded arithmetic) and `CKKSFloat`, serialized little-endian (`scalar_bytes`, `to_le_bytes`), never platform math.
- **Setup-math pins.** A change to `CKKSFloat`, the quantize codecs, the root table or the `libm` or `astro-float-num` versions keeps `setup_math_golden_vectors` and the fixtures or updates and explains them, and `poulpy-ckks/src/numerics/cos_quadrant_f128.bin` comes only from `regenerate_quadrant_tables`, checked by `quadrant_tables_match_generator` (`docs/review/workspace.md` section 1).

## 16. Do not report

In addition to `docs/review/workspace.md` section 18:

- The prepared dot product's `InvalidOperand`, or the ciphertext dot product's eager fallback, past `base2k = 62` reported as a radix rejected for a sum's length (section 7).
- A request for byte-for-byte parity of float encoding-transform outputs: the determinism fixtures check byte identity (section 15.4).
- A functional op test that does not check evaluation outputs untagged: parity's `assert_untagged` does (section 15.2).
