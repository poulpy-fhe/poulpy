# poulpy-ckks review rules

## 1. Layering, OEP families and macros

- **Register a new family everywhere.** A new OEP family joins in the same diff:
  - `impl_cpu_ckks_defaults!` (`backends/poulpy-cpu-portable/src/backend_defaults.rs`) and `impl_oracle_ckks!` (`backends/poulpy-cpu-oracle/src/ckks.rs`) when it serves both rings and no backend specializes it (else per type);
  - when standard-only, every standard backend type's standard lists, Rayon twins included, and `impl_oracle_ckks_standard!`, never `impl_cpu_ckks_defaults!` or the umbrella `impl_ckks_reference_full!`;
  - for a scalar-independent evaluation family, the supertraits of `CKKSImpl` (`schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/oep/ckks_impl.rs`) and its blanket impl's `where` clause, which never take a scalar-generic, fold, bootstrapping or ModUp seam, `CKKSImpl` staying the crate's only blanket `*Impl` impl;
  - the test-only fixture backends whose tests use it.
- **Reference family shape.** A family's reference is `pub trait CKKS<Family>Reference<BE: Backend>` of default-bodied `<api name>_reference` and `<api query>_reference` methods with their own `where Self:` bounds and an empty `Module<BE>` impl.
- **Derived bodies.** A same-layer composition is a provided OEP method calling one `<op>_derived`.
- **Scalar-generic seams.** Anything generic over the encoding scalar `F` is its own OEP trait (`CKKSEncodingImpl<F>`, `CKKSDFTMatrixImpl<F>`) or a method bounded by `Self: CKKSEncodingImpl<F>` (PaCo and SHIP coefficient encodings), never a parameter of `CKKSImpl` or a scalar-independent family; its API trait takes `F` as a trait parameter, its delegate impl, not its methods, requires the seam, and its reference macro covers every `F` of the backend's `CKKSEncodingImpl<F>`.
- **Core through its public seams.** A CKKS reference reaches core through its bounded core API traits and queries, pure sizing helpers, API-less helpers (`GGLWEProductReference`) and the `DiagonalProd` hook with `glwe_accumulate_streamed_baby_steps_dft`, a missing core seam being added in core.
- **Containers hold data, modules compute.** Computations are `Module<BE>` traits, and methods of `CKKSCiphertext`, `CKKSPlaintext` and their views only manage data (`set_meta_checked`, `to_ref`) or are host-named codecs.

## 2. Naming, signatures and argument order

- **Operation grammar.** A new operation is `ckks_<op>[_<qualifier>](res, ..)`, its in-place form `_assign`, and its OEP method, API-less seams included, shares its name.
- **Operand-kind qualifiers.** The qualifier names the second operand: `_pt_vec` a plaintext polynomial, `_pt_const` one plaintext coefficient, `_ct` a ciphertext beside plaintext forms, `_prepared` a prepared operand, `_host` a host adapter; a constant is never a "cleartext".
- **Operand names.** A new operation names its source `a`, binary operands `a` and `b`, plaintext `pt` (indices `pt_coeff`, `res_coeff`), term slices `a` and `b` or `srcs`, `babies` and `lts`, keys `keys` and context `ctx`.
- **Argument order.** After `&self` come per-call scalars (`k`, `bits`, indices, Galois element), destinations, sources, caches, plans, contexts and matrices (`babies` before `lt`), keys, workers, then `scratch`, each coefficient selector right after its operand (`ckks_add_pt_const(res, res_coeff, a, pt, pt_coeff, scratch)`, products without `res_coeff`), and an out-of-place form is its `_assign` form with sources inserted after `res` and its selector.
- **Encryption and setup order.** Encryption is `ckks_encrypt_sk(res, pt, sk, source_xe, source_xa, scratch)`, decryption `ckks_decrypt(res, a, sk, scratch)`, and setup takes its module first after `self`.
- **Operand bounds.** A new operation bounds its result by `GLWEToBackendMut<BE> + CKKSCtBounds + SetCKKSInfos`, ciphertext sources by `GLWEToBackendRef<BE> + CKKSCtBounds`, plaintexts also by `IntPolyInfos`, keys by `GetTensorKey<BE>` or `GetAutomorphismKey<BE>` and query key infos by `GGLWEInfos`, never a concrete `CKKSCiphertext`.

## 3. Errors and validation order

- **Typed variants for caller misuse.** A caller-fixable condition returns a `CKKSCompositionError` through the crate helpers, a recurring new one getting a helper or variant:
  - `checked_log_budget_sub` (`InsufficientHomomorphicCapacity`), `checked_mul_ct_log_budget` and `checked_mul_pt_log_budget` (`MultiplicationPrecisionUnderflow`) for the budget;
  - `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_*`, `ensure_encryption_degrees`, `check_prepared_layout` (`PreparedOperandLayoutMismatch`), `set_meta_checked` (`LimbReallocationShrinksBelowMetadata`) and `CoeffsMeta::ensure_width` for operands;
  - `MissingAutomorphismKey` or `MissingRelinearizationKey`, with the lookup width and the source's `CoreError`, for keys;
  - otherwise `InvalidOperand`, `InvalidPlan` only for a plan literal, `DftMatrixMismatch` for a wrong matrix.
- **Adding a variant.** `CKKSError` and `CKKSCompositionError` stay `#[non_exhaustive]`, and a new variant serves a condition callers branch on that `InvalidOperand` cannot, carries `op` (or `plan`) and the explaining numbers and returns a wrapped error from `Error::source`.
- **The op label.** The `op` of an error or helper, which also starts a `ckks_ensure!` message, is the invoked `ckks_` public method, or its stem without `_assign` when one body serves both; a helper shared by several methods takes the caller's `op`, and a body several public methods reach through a fixed OEP signature labels its primary public method.
- **Setup errors keep the chain.** Setup returns `anyhow::Result` holding typed errors (`InvalidPlan { plan: "<TypeName>", .. }.into()`), adding context with `.context`, `.with_context` or `?`, never by formatting the error.
- **What may panic.** A fallible operation returns errors for caller input, panicking only in a nested scratch check, while allocation-class functions and layout queries (`*_alloc*`, `*_bytes_of*`, `take_*_scratch`, infallible `*_tmp_bytes`, `*_layout`, `*_output_k`, Galois element lists) panic with a documented message.
- **Validate, stamp, then work.** A fallible body checks every term, lane, output and worker (slice lengths, equal and non-zero, then layouts, scales and slot kinds, keys, budget, worker scratch), then stamps, then computes, `res` staying untouched on `Err` outside a documented partial-write path.
- **Errors in the docs.** A new or changed fallible API method lists under `# Errors` each variant it returns and when (or "As [`Self::other`]."), the eager `ckks_dot_product_ct` documenting that it fails after a write.
- **Plans validate once.** A setup type with invariants keeps private or `pub(crate)` fields behind constructors and builders (`new`, `from_*`, `with_*`, `compile_*`) that validate before allocating, `with_*` resetting the memoized values it affects, a struct-literal plan validates once at its compile entry (`EvalModPlan::validate`), invalid shapes are made unrepresentable (paired steps, not parallel vectors), derived plan queries stay infallible and evaluators re-check only plan-versus-runtime clauses.
- **Plan literals carry `CoeffsMeta`.** A plan literal carries `CoeffsMeta`, never a layout, and every encoder allocating from one calls `CoeffsMeta::ensure_width(op)` first.

## 4. Scratch, allocation and prepared operands

- **Temporaries come from scratch.** A leveled operation carves its temporary ciphertexts and plaintexts in `scratch.scope(..)`, the heap holding only caller-kept objects and commented per-call outputs whose count the plan sets or that cross threads.
- **Allocation goes through `CKKSModuleAlloc`.** Ciphertexts, plaintexts and linear transformations come from default methods of `CKKSModuleAlloc` (empty `Module<BE>` impl), size first (none for a ciphertext, at the module degree), then `base2k`, `k`, `rank`, `_from_infos` copying the CKKS metadata and `_from_glwe_infos` dropping it.
- **Prepared-operand lifecycle.** A CKKS prepared type is filled by `ckks_prepare_<side>(res, a, scratch)`, which refuses another degree, radix, width or rank with `InvalidOperand`; its infos carry the source's layout and metadata, and products check it against `res` with `check_prepared_layout`.
- **Caller-owned modules, workers and outputs.** Host-side setup takes the caller's `&Module<HostBytesBackend<R>>` and transfers explicitly, pipeline outputs are caller-allocated, and parallel PaCo checks every `PaCoWorker`'s degree and scratch against its own module before any branch, recombining in increasing order.
- **One query per operation stem.** A scratch-taking operation has one `<op stem>_tmp_bytes` shared by its out-of-place and `_assign` forms, unless the `_assign` form has its own query, `_assign` callers passing `res` as result and operand, or the family query its doc names (`ckks_prepare_tmp_bytes` for `ckks_prepare_left` and `ckks_prepare_right`, `ckks_dft_tmp_bytes` for DFT format wrappers), and no query outside the limb-wise families copies their `res_size: usize` form.
- **An `_assign` copy.** An `_assign` needing the old `res` copies it with `glwe_copy` into a scoped temporary and runs the out-of-place form.
- **Queries cover every route.** A query maximizes over every runtime route (one-pass or tensor, prepared or streamed diagonals, lazy or eager sums, every rotation-key layout, the work layout and the caller's destination), sizing a nested call on the caller's destination at its allocation (`max_k`) and a temporary at its carve width (`res.k()`).
- **Concurrent stages split the arena.** A reference running stages through `BE::TaskExecutor::join` splits the arena under the same `TaskExecutor::IS_PARALLEL` predicate as its query's stage count times `worker_scratch_bytes::<BE>(stage query)` reservation, its serial branch running the same stages in order.
- **Staged compilation.** A compiled context whose preparation budget depends on generated material offers `compile_unprepared`, `prepare_tmp_bytes` from the selected queries and a freeing `prepare`, beside a one-shot `compile` documented to cover both budgets.
- **Pipeline queries validate.** PaCo and SHIP `_tmp_bytes` queries return `CKKSResult<usize>` after runtime validation.

## 5. Ring, slots and keys

- **Standard-only families by type.** A family or composition needing complex slots or standard-ring automorphisms (conjugation, multiplication by `i`, homomorphic DFT, bootstrapping and ModUp, fold, PaCo and SHIP coefficient encodings, the standard side of the ring maps) bounds `Backend<Ring = Standard>` on its trait or impl, directly or through a supertrait.
- **The ring is a parameter.** CKKS containers carry their ring as a type parameter, implementing backend conversions only for `R = BE::Ring`, and ring-generic signatures name it (`CKKSFoldOps<BE, R>`).
- **Slots and Galois elements.** Ring-shared code takes slot counts from `ckks_max_slots` and `ckks_slots_of_degree` and rotations from `ckks_galois_element(k)`, conjugating with `-1`.
- **Identity rotations are keyless.** A rotation by Galois element 1, baby and giant step 0 included, is a copy or direct fold that looks up no key, and key sets omit element 1.
- **Key widths at lookup.** Keys are looked up, sized and pre-checked at the width the switch decomposes: `min(a.k(), res.k())` for an out-of-place rotation or conjugation (a narrower `res` receiving the source through the aligning shift first, so core decomposes at that width), `res.k()` for `_assign`, the widest operand (`res` included for `_assign`) for a tensor product, `a.k()` for baby steps, the output width for giant steps, the branch width in PaCo, the width model's stage width in bootstrapping.
- **Key sets from the schedule.** A key-consuming pipeline derives its `galois_elements` query from the schedule it evaluates (or a replay sharing it), key generation, validation and docs using it instead of a hand-kept list.
- **Row-offset keys.** A switch using a row offset checks `check_offset_raise` before any work.
- **Shared key validators.** Pipelines validate gadget keys, reported key metadata and storage capacity before any work with the `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/layouts/validation.rs` helpers, checking both the reported infos and the backend view (`validate_gadget_key` and `validate_gadget_backend_view`, `validate_storage_capacity` and `validate_backend_storage_capacity`).

## 6. Metadata and stamping

- **The observable fields.** Every operation, overrides included, stamps all output metadata itself (`k`, base2k, rank, scale, `SlotsKind`, `log_sparsity`), `log_budget` being derived as `k - log_delta` and no CKKS type storing a budget or its own metadata struct.
- **No rescale operation.** No rescale operation exists or is added: products rescale through `cnv_offset` (to `min(log_delta)`, or the ciphertext's against a plaintext), chained transforms carry none between factors, results land at `res.k()`, never at the limb-aligned allocation, and only `ckks_div_pow2_into` and `ckks_div_pow2_assign` move budget bits under `log_delta`.
- **Stamp before the rounding write.** Offsets are computed against the pre-stamp `res` and the final metadata stamped before any step rounding into `res`, except:
  - an `_assign` product reading `res` stamps after its apply step and before relinearization (`StampOrder::AfterApply`) or works from a copy;
  - a one-pass product taking the output width as an argument stamps right after the call;
  - lazy flag-clearing outputs, `set_slots` and `set_log_sparsity` may follow the write.
- **One shared formula per rule.** Offsets, budgets and `cnv_offset` come only from the shared helpers: `ckks_shift_stamp_unary` or `ckks_copy_stamp_unary` for unary shift-and-relabel, `ckks_offset_binary` (`min(log_delta) + min(log_budget) - res.k()`, saturating) for add and sub, `get_mul_ct_params` or `mul_ct_params_raw` for ciphertext products, and `get_mul_pt_params` or `mul_pt_params_raw` for every plaintext product (diagonals, fused BSGS combinations, SHIP masks); a fused alignment repeats `ckks_offset_unary`, `checked_log_budget_sub`, `set_meta(a.meta())`, `set_log_budget`, then the kernel, on the direct path when `ckks_unary_exact` holds.
- **Relabel helpers by accounting.** Copying a source's labels is `set_meta(a.meta())`, `set_log_budget(..)`, then `set_log_delta` if the scale moves, and a lift spending budget at a fixed modulus changes the scale through the meta, never `set_log_delta`.
- **Slot kinds.** Every operation stamps its `SlotsKind` (`Integer < Real < Complex`) by these rules, never finer, refusing `Integer` input if it can break integrality (`div_pow2`):
  - the operands' `join` for sums, products, dot products and linear transformations, diagonals included;
  - `Complex` after multiplying or dividing by `i` or adding a constant at a nonzero coefficient;
  - `Real` or a `meet` only where an identity proves it (`z + conj(z)`, traces, conjugate-invariant ring maps, real-valued polynomials, EvalMod, LUT and bootstrap outputs, the slot transform's real and imaginary halves), encoding using `meet(BE::Ring::SLOTS)`.
- **Sparsity composition.** `log_sparsity` is the operands' minimum (`log2(N / n)` for a degree-`n` plaintext, `lt_log_sparsity` for a diagonal), and an operation moving coefficient positions sets it explicitly: repack moves it by one, merging distinct inputs clears it, a PaCo branch stamps its gap.
- **Noise metadata.** `div_pow2_assign`, identity rotation, one-term `add_many` and the DFT chain clear the noise estimate, only a zero-offset copy keeps it, re-ranked to the destination, and encryption records `ComponentNoise::from_secret_at` last.
- **Encryption stamps, decryption does not.** Encryption stamps the plaintext's `log_delta`, slot kind and `log2(N / n)` sparsity with `log_budget = k - log_delta`, normalizing once, and `ckks_decrypt` and `ckks_extract_pt` write coefficients only, at the preset alignment (`PlaintextAlignmentImpossible`), into degree `n < N` only when `log2(N / n) <= ct.log_sparsity()` (`PlaintextDegreeMismatch`).
- **Copy CKKS values with `ckks_copy`.** A CKKS result moves into a CKKS destination with `ckks_copy`, `glwe_copy` only into one already carrying the intended label or relabelled right after.
- **Output queries feed the reference.** For a public output query (`ckks_linear_transformation_output_k`, `ckks_fold_layout`), the reference derives its output from it or debug-asserts agreement.
- **Document the metadata rule.** A stamping API trait documents under `# Metadata` its `offset`, `log_delta_out` and `log_budget_out` formulas per differing variant family, plus slot kind, sparsity, flag effect and preconditions with their errors.

## 7. Laziness and normalization

- **Lazy linear operations.** Additions and subtractions, plaintext forms included, and `ckks_double_into` write raw limb sums and clear the flag after validation; negation, exact copies, multiplication or division by `i` and `div_pow2_assign` keep it; products, rotations, conjugation, key switches and decryption output canonical values; linear operations normalize only through an aligning shift.
- **Raw limb shortcuts.** A limb-wise operation skips the normalizing `glwe_lsh` shift only when `ckks_unary_exact` or `ckks_holds_limbs` holds.
- **Manual canonical flags.** A poulpy-ckks body sets the flag only after a normalization of every column, as `set_canonical(a.is_canonical())` on exactly copied or permuted digits, or, commented, after a stamp lowered `k` on a value canonical at the new width.
- **Shared values normalized once.** Power-basis entries and the lazy tensor sum before relinearization are normalized once where produced, a per-term sum normalization commenting its bound.
- **Lazy sums.** Summed tensor products keep `base2k <= 62`, past which the ciphertext dot product goes eager and the prepared one returns `InvalidOperand`; a long CKKS sum never rejects a term count for its length.

## 8. Products and DFT economy

- **Prepare a reused operand once.** An operand several products read in a row (a giant-step power, SHIP `pi` plaintexts, a diagonal set) is prepared once and kept until its last product, counted in the query or documented as a heap-owned prepared object.
- **Stages at their narrowest width.** A pipeline runs each stage at the width the remaining circuit needs: products and transforms at `res.k()`, the DFT chain factor by factor in place, a PaCo accumulator at `output_k + circuit_depth`.
- **Fused paths fall back.** The gated fused paths (BSGS baby-step combination, one-pass product and relinearization, lazy tensor sum) fall back to the composed per-term path.

## 9. Plaintexts and encoding

- **Plaintexts by slot count.** A message of `m` slots is allocated with `ckks_pt_vec_alloc(m, base2k, k)`, at degree `2m` on the standard ring and `m` on the conjugate-invariant ring, panicking below `MIN_DEGREE`, a decryption target in tests allowed at `ckks_plaintext_alloc(ct.n(), ..)`.
- **Plaintext operand checks.** Before any write, a `_pt_vec` operation runs `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_degree_embeds(op, ct_n, pt_n, BE::MIN_DEGREE)` and `ensure_plaintext_alignment`, and a `_pt_const` one, with no degree check, runs `ensure_base2k_match`, `ensure_plaintext_coeff_in_range` for each index against its own operand's degree (`"ciphertext"`, `"plaintext"`) and `ensure_plaintext_alignment`.
- **One value per coefficient.** Encoders and decoders take exactly one value per plaintext coefficient or `ckks_slots_of_degree(n)` planar `[re | im]` slots (`2m` scalars for `m` slots), returning an error for any other count and never spreading values at a gap.
- **Quantization rejects before writing.** Every encoding path, overrides included, quantizes through `ckks_quantize` or `ckks_quantize_i64`, never an `as` cast, rejecting a non-finite value or one outside the codec range (`i64` while `log_delta + log_budget <= 63`, else `i128`) before any write, and keeps the preset metadata, narrowing only the slot kind with `meet`.
- **Resident encoding signatures.** Encoding API and OEP signatures take only backend-resident buffers (`CKKSEncodingBuffer`) and plaintext views, host slices entering only through host-named adapters (`CKKSEncodingHostOps`) staging through the caller's arena with `copy_host_into_encoding_buffer` and `copy_encoding_buffer_into_host`.
- **Encoding plans in the cache.** Encoding plans are backend-owned, cached per scalar (`ckks_encoding_plan_cache`, built by `ckks_encoding_plans_create`) once per key, never rebuilt per call or kept in a thread-local, and encoding operations take no plan argument.

## 10. Numerics and determinism

- **Setup math through `CKKSFloat`.** Production code calls no `f32`, `f64` or `num_traits::Float` transcendental, `sqrt`, `powi`, `powf`, `cbrt` or `hypot`: setup values are computed in the working scalar `F` through `CKKSFloat`, irrational constants never pass through `f64`, a wider intermediate narrows to `F` mantissa-exactly, and a new transcendental joins `CKKSFloat` (libm for `f32` and `f64`, portable binary128 for `Quad`) with `f32`, `f64` and `num_traits::Float` entries in `schemes/poulpy-ckks/clippy.toml`, none for `f128`.
- **`Quad` math by hand.** `Quad` math outside `scalar::backing` goes through `Float` or `CKKSFloat`, never the inner `f128`; only `scalar::backing` picks the backing (`CKKSFloat` always the portable one), and `libquadmath` changes no stored bit, codec or type.
- **Platform-math allows.** Outside test code (`cfg(test)` and the `test-utils` `test_suite`), a `clippy::disallowed_methods` suppression marks only a deliberate platform-math route.
- **libquadmath stays allowlisted.** The `f128` crate appears only in the x86_64 Linux GNU target tables of `schemes/poulpy-ckks/Cargo.toml`, behind the non-default `libquadmath` feature or as a parity-test dev-dependency, the pure-Rust `astro-float-num` and `libm` backing staying on every target.
- **Byte-identical encoding.** Encoding, decoding (PaCo and SHIP coefficient encodings included) and bootstrapping setup constants are byte-identical on every backend and platform at each precision in both rings, `CKKSEncodingImpl` overrides following the portable arithmetic (`docs/review/backends.md` section 5.9) under the determinism fixtures.
- **The oracle derives roots independently.** A change to `CKKSFloat::ckks_root_of_unity`, `ROOT_TABLE_LOG_ORDER` or the slot-transform butterflies or their rounding is matched in `backends/poulpy-cpu-oracle/src/ckks_roots.rs` and `ckks_fft.rs`, neither side adjusted to the other's values.
- **Sealed PaCo and SHIP scalars.** PaCo and SHIP encodings accept only the sealed `PaCoScalar` and `ShipScalar` (`f64`, `Quad`), never unsealed or extended to `f32`, rejecting `log_q >= MANTISSA_BITS` (PaCo) or `base2k >= MANTISSA_BITS` (SHIP).

## 11. Linear transformations and diagonals

- **Scale-agnostic engines live in core.** CKKS linear-transformation and polynomial-evaluation code holds only scale, budget and `cnv_offset` accounting, encoding and the Galois-to-key map, scale-free machinery going to poulpy-core.
- **A diagonal stores one period.** `ComplexDiagonals` entries follow `docs/review/core.md` section 8.4 (diagonal periods), storing the shortest predictable period, an absent part counting 1 in `period_lcm`, and a bit-reversed layer is replicated to the slot count first.
- **One period per transformation.** `build_transform` hands over every diagonal at the map period `ComplexDiagonals::period()`, encoded at its `period_degree` (`2 * period` standard, `period` invariant), a period whose degree is below the backend floor repeated, and callers size the encoders with `ckks_encode_linear_transformation_tmp_bytes(module, p)` at that period or a bound, never from a ciphertext layout.
- **Uniform diagonals.** Encoders, hand-built transform paths included, produce diagonals of one scale, width, slot kind and degree, and every consumer of a caller-built transformation, the prepare included, checks scale, width and slot kind in release with `ensure_uniform_diagonals`.
- **Summed transforms check every term.** A summed linear transformation checks every term against the first (schedule, diagonal layout, width, scale, slot kind, source layout and scale), each baby cache's rotation coverage before reading its layout, and every key.
- **Every BSGS path stays selectable.** DFT factors take their giant step from the plan's schedule, `optimal_bsgs_giant_step` and `DFTPlan::with_optimal_bsgs` stay opt-in, and `Direct`, `Bsgs`, prepared or streamed diagonals, PaCo slot orders and psi tails, C2S-first, S2C-first, EvalRound+ and the raise's first factor stay caller-selectable.
- **One DFT generator.** Every homomorphic DFT factor comes from `gen_dft_matrices` or `gen_dft_matrices_blockwise` under the crate's conventions (generator-5 embedding, natural slot order), scheme conventions (bit reversal, slot order, fused masks) being post-transforms of those factors (`compose`), never a private factor stack or FFT.
- **Replays share the generator's geometry.** Value-free replays (`DFTPlan::diagonal_indexes`, `galois_elements`) share the generator's `dft_layer_*` helpers.
- **Schedules in evaluation order.** A `DFTPlan` schedule lists factors in evaluation order for `Encode` and `Decode` alike, never reordered by kind, an inverting Decode using the reversed schedule the caller passes.
- **Direction and format are types.** A DFT matrix carries direction and format as sealed type markers, each evaluation entry takes its exact `DFTMatrix<BE, Dir, Fmt, ..>`, and only `ckks_new_dft_matrix` resolves them at runtime, validating hand-built factors and refusing a dense `RepackImagAsReal` with `DftMatrixMismatch`.

## 12. Bootstrapping, PaCo and SHIP

- **Bootstrap entry checks.** `ckks_bootstrap_reference` and `ckks_functional_bootstrap_reference` check before any work the context kind, rank 1, encapsulation keys exactly when the recipe encapsulates, and every key's coverage of the run's widths (`check_key_widths`, `check_raise_keys`), new key-consuming stages included, only the EvalRound+ bypass relying on stage lookups.
- **One key-width model.** Preset key layouts, entry checks and stage-width computations derive widths only from `BootstrappingPlan::key_widths()`, which a change of a stage's working width updates, EvalRound+ bypass keys staying outside it at the raised-width shape, and the bootstrap scratch query maximizes over every rotation-key layout at the widest ciphertexts.
- **Preset names.** A bootstrapping preset is named `n{log_n}_d{log_delta}_k{output_k}_p{log2_precision}_{c2s|s2c}` plus the technique suffixes of `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/presets/bootstrapping.rs`, renamed when `k` or `p` changes; a test-only preset is `insecure_`-prefixed, `pub(crate)` and gated on `test-utils`.
- **Registering a preset.** A public preset `pub fn <name>() -> anyhow::Result<BootstrappingPreset>` calls `build(PresetSpec { name: "<name>", .. })` with its measured `log2_precision` rounded down and lands in `presets::bootstrapping::all()` in stable order, `all_lists_every_preset_once`, a `<name>_is_composable_and_within_bounds` test pinning its widths, consumed bits and key shapes, and a doc comment and `docs/guides/bootstrapping.md` row stating those widths; `bootstrapping_presets_meet_precision` checks both real and imaginary slots, and an `insecure_` preset stays out of `all()` with its own default-run test.
- **Preset key shapes are derived.** `build` derives every preset key layout from its use through `key_layout`, `widest_k_digit` and `key_fits` (whole-limb digits in the fewest rows the modulus bound admits, the `GGLWELayout::dnum_for_input` digit count, a `k_aux` guard of at least one digit plus `KEY_NOISE_MARGIN`, each key covering its stage's widest input), `with_dsizes` being the only override, under the same fit and modulus validation.
- **Preset modulus bounds.** Every preset declares a cited modulus bound per secret (dense and sparse), which `validate_modulus_bounds` checks from `build`, `with_base2k` and `with_dsizes` against the bootstrap width and every key layout's gadget, auxiliary and total modulus, each under its own secret's bound.
- **PaCo slot order.** `PaCoSlotOrder` defaults to `Natural` and stays stable for persisted keys, and a convention change touches only its relabel sites (`paco_c2s_factors`, the packing gather, `paco_stc_factors`) behind the flag.
- **PaCo and SHIP hooks.** `CKKSPaCoCoeffEncodingImpl` and `CKKSShipCoeffEncodingImpl` are required hooks with no reference macro, byte-identical with their `*_host` definitions on every backend; the PaCo and SHIP drivers and stages follow section 1 like every other family.
- **Key-bundle checks are structural.** PaCo and SHIP key-bundle constructors and preflight validate structure only (layouts, metadata, Galois labels, gadget dimensions, capacity, fingerprint).
- **A new fold ring.** A new fold input ring implements the crate-private `FoldRing<BE>` step, reusing the shared fold and unfold body.

## 13. Polynomial evaluation and approximation

- **Depth from core's model.** Degree and depth selection uses core's `bsgs_eval_depth` and `BSGSPolynomial::eval_depth`, never a local formula.
- **`consumed_bits` is the budget prediction.** Every compiled plan or approximation exposes `consumed_bits()` and its depth from its evaluation's accounting, the evaluation checks `log_budget >= consumed_bits()` or predicts its output metadata before any write, a predicting pipeline re-checks the evaluated width at the end, and budget changes update `consumed_bits()` and its pinned tests.
- **Shared scratch bound.** Polynomial evaluation has no query of its own, callers sizing it by `ckks_all_ops_tmp_bytes` or `ckks_all_ops_with_atk_tmp_bytes`, so a new callee, larger callee query or BSGS temporary in it, its `BSGSOps` implementation, the power basis or the approximation layer updates the `CKKSAllOpsTmpBytes` maximum, and EvalMod's query when EvalMod reaches it; DFT and EvalMod queries stay outside that maximum.
- **Approximation entry points.** A host fitting or selection entry point comes as `name` (default `RemezOptions`) and `name_with(.., opts)` over one `*_multi_interval_with` body, validates every scalar argument first (finite, positive, ordered disjoint intervals, nonzero options), documents callbacks as pure and reads Remez samples from its per-fit cache.

## 14. Containers, secrets and serialization

- **`CKKSInfos` only on CKKS containers.** `CKKSInfos`, hence `SetCKKSInfos` and `CKKSCtBounds`, is implemented only on CKKS containers, views, prepared operands, `CKKSLayout` and crate-private scratch infos, never a core type, `SetCKKSInfos` staying blanket-implemented.
- **New scratch-backed views.** A scratch-backed CKKS container is a `view_wrapper!`-style struct, one `impl_ckks_infos!` (`ckks_inner` around a CKKS container, `glwe_inner` around a GLWE view), `GLWEToBackendRef` and `GLWEToBackendMut` and a `ScratchArenaTakeCKKS` carve, a flagless plaintext view making `set_noise` a no-op, and a container dereferencing to a core type implements `Clone` by hand, bounded on the inner `Clone`.
- **Setup-created secrets are encapsulated.** A secret that CKKS setup creates or derives is drawn from a caller-passed source and tagged `Distribution::ENCAPSULATED("<name>")`, an ephemeral one never leaving its call.
- **Only `CKKSCiphertext` serializes.** `CKKSCiphertext` alone serializes, as its GLWE with no CKKS header, plaintext metadata included, and key bundles store core key types.
- **Module visibility.** The `oep` family modules stay private behind re-exports of each `*Impl` trait and macro, `error` private behind root re-exports of `CKKSError`, `CKKSCompositionError` and `CKKSResult` and `pub(crate)` ones of `ckks_ensure!`, `ckks_bail!` and its helpers, and the pipeline submodules (PaCo context, keyset, plan, secret) `pub(crate)` behind re-exports of their public types; the root re-exports the core metadata types of CKKS signatures, and `reference` is `pub`.

## 15. Tests and registration

### 15.1. Registration

- **Register every suite test.** A new functional `pub fn test_*` in `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/<module>.rs` joins the `ckks_backend_test_suite!` list of `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/mod.rs` without its `test_` prefix (a second registration with another argument under its own descriptive name), through `run_test_result!` for `anyhow::Result<()>`, `run_test_with_arg!` for a trailing argument, `run_params_test!` for modules built from `params`, `run_test_ignored!` for a paper-scale or too-slow case beside its enabled small case, else `run_test!`, or, when its signature does not fit, from the macro body, a new module being declared `pub mod <name>;` there.
- **Suite test signatures.** An independent-suite test is `pub fn test_<name><BE, F, E>(params: CKKSTestParams, module: &Module<BE>, host_module: &Module<HostBytesBackend>)` with `BE: TestContextBackend<Ring = Standard>` and `F: TestScalar`, and a parity helper `pub fn test_<area>_parity<BR, BT, F>(params, module_ref, module_test)`.
- **Rank-2 coverage.** A rank-generic operation registers at least one test also in `ckks_backend_rank2_test_suite!` under the same name, reading the rank from `params`, never `Rank(1)`.
- **Conjugate-invariant tests.** Conjugate-invariant tests are one `#[test]` per case inside `conjugate_invariant_ckks_test_suite!`, each building its own modules (invariant at `params.n`, standard sibling at `2n` or `4n`).
- **Shared helpers stay ring-generic.** `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/helpers.rs` helpers bound only `BE: TestContextBackend` (or a transfer bound) and `Module<BE>: TestContextSharedModule<BE>`, taking `&Module<HostBytesBackend<BE::Ring>>`.
- **Parity helpers on every backend.** A parity helper in `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/parity/`, re-exported from its `mod.rs`, joins these `ckks_parity_test_suite!` lists of every backend crate running CKKS parity, oracle included, or, for one backend's own path, of that backend (`docs/review/backends.md` section 13.2):
  - the `_f64` and `_quad` lists;
  - the `ckks_parity_ci_*` lists unless a bound, direct or through an OEP supertrait, needs `Ring = Standard`;
  - the standard and invariant rank-2 lists if rank-generic;
  - the `f32` lists, where kept, for encoding or DFT-matrix generation at `F`, never PaCo or SHIP encoding.
- **CI tokens.** A check meant for the native AVX-512 lane (tokens `ckks_parity`, `determinism`) or the manual NEON step (`ckks_parity`) is a parity helper registered under a `ckks_parity_[ci_]<backend>_<case>` module or a fixture test whose path contains `determinism`.

### 15.2. Parity

- **Two suites, two jobs.** The independent suite checks cleartext expectations, the noise model and precision bounds, never comparing backends; a parity helper checks only bit-exact agreement, never a cleartext expectation or noise bound, using no secret key except encryption parity's fixed-digit secret, and a check needing opaque storage is a parity helper.
- **Parity drivers stay opaque.** Parity helpers and determinism drivers bound only `Backend<ZnxWord = i64>`, the traits they call and transfer bounds such as `HostStaged`.
- **Shape of a parity helper.** A helper runs one deterministic driver per backend on `fixture_*` inputs and `parity::keys`, returning `Snapshot`s of every output, then calls `assert_eq!`.
- **Parity sweeps the metadata.** A driver sweeps each metadata dimension its operation reads or stamps (narrower and wider destinations, off-grid widths, zero and positive `log_sparsity`, every stamped slot kind, unequal scales, smaller-degree plaintexts, the rank of `params`) and, for an operation reading through a DFT, lazy and canonical operands (`fixture_operand(.., lazy)`), flagging raw-digit fixtures as their digits are.
- **Prepared storage is never compared.** Prepared operands, DFT matrices and encoding plans are compared across backends only through their consumers' results, diagonal plaintexts through `diagonal_snapshot`, and prepared bytes only within one backend (prepare-into against prepare).
- **Encoding parity is exact.** Encoding transforms, slot-composition methods, the coefficient codec and PaCo and SHIP embeddings compare across backends byte for byte, sign of zero included (the codec by `Snapshot`), never within a tolerance.
- **Encryption parity.** Encryption parity registers only through `ckks_encryption_parity_test_suite!`.

### 15.3. Functional tests

- **Exact budget per call.** The operation under test runs in `parity::helpers::with_scratch` (polynomial evaluation, which has no query, at the `ckks_all_ops_tmp_bytes` bound of section 13), the shared `alloc_scratch` arena serving only setup (keys, encryption, decryption).
- **Decrypt through the checked helpers.** Tests decrypt through `assert_decrypt_precision`, `assert_decrypt_precision_at_log_delta` (when a lower-scale operand bounds the precision), `ckks_decrypt_with_prec`, `ckks_decrypt_decode` or a file-local helper calling `assert_canonical_at_k` first, never a bare `ckks_decrypt`; exact standard-ring operations compare against expected values from the quantized inputs (`quantize`, `quantized_const`, `quantized_vector`), never an absolute tolerance, and approximating pipelines use `precision_stats`.
- **Floors document their measurement.** A test's precision or noise floor (`MIN_AVG_LOG2_PREC`, `NOISE_GUARD_BITS`, `PRECISION_GUARD_BITS`, a noise bound) states its measurement or model and margin, and moves only with a new measurement.
- **Output metadata from the inputs.** Tests assert output metadata against values computed from the inputs and the destination's `k` before the call (`assert_*_output_meta`, with `a.k()` and a `ckks_snapshot` taken before mutation for `_assign`, `assert_ct_meta` for composites), and leveled pipelines their exact `log_budget` use against `consumed_bits()` and the preserved `log_delta`.
- **Noise tags in op tests.** Encryption tests assert the recorded fresh estimate (`precision() == ct.k()`, parties, rank, distribution, sigma) and the error's measured second moment against the model, and copy tests pin that an equal-width `ckks_copy` keeps the estimate while a narrowing copy, a one-term `add_many` and a prepared operand carry none.
- **Rejection tests pin the variant.** A rejection test pins the `CKKSCompositionError` variant and `op` (`assert_ckks_error`, or `matches!` on `err.composition()`) and, for a documented order, breaks two checks and asserts the earlier; a parity driver may assert only `is_err()` with an unchanged snapshot when the independent test pins the variant.
- **Arithmetic case matrix.** A new leveled out-of-place and `_assign` operation has `_aligned`, `_smaller_output` (off-grid at `k - base2k - 1`) and `_assign` tests, a second operand adding `_delta_log_delta` (lower scale) and `_delta_a_lt_b`, `_delta_a_gt_b`, `_assign_self_lt`, `_assign_self_gt` (budgets a limb apart), plus one `_rejects_<case>` test per typed rejection.
- **Paths stay pinned.** A test named after an internal path asserts the condition selecting it, and a runtime predicate choosing circuits is tested on both sides and at its boundary.
- **Fused forms equal their composition.** A fused, summed, prepared, one-shot, view-input or parallel form and an orchestrator equal by `snapshot` (digits, layout, flag, noise tag) the composition or form their contract names.
- **Keys in tests.** Test keys use `ksk_layout(k_in)` for a wider input, and a diff touching key lookup or width selection pins the looked-up width with `TensorKeyAt` or `QueryLog`.
- **Transforms against independent references.** A transform is tested in each direction against a reference independent of production code (`ReferenceEncoder`, a cleartext model, `ComplexDiagonals::evaluate`), not only by a round trip, the independent suite encoding inputs and expected values on the host with `ReferenceEncoder`.
- **Prepare-into lifecycle tests.** A prepare-into (or encode-into) object is tested per side and `PrepareHint` for `bytes_of` equal to its allocation, prepare-into equal to prepare, a `0xA5`-poisoned refill matching without reallocating, and another layout refused with `InvalidOperand`, bytes and infos unchanged.
- **Seeds per call.** Test encryption and key-generation helpers (`ckks_encrypt*`, `gen_tsk`, `gen_atk`, `gen_switching_key`, `gen_ci_keys`) draw from `next_test_seed(tag)` sources, a fixed `Source::new([x; 32])` serving only secret keys, messages, parity fixtures, single draws and unconsumed-source rejection tests.
- **Test degrees and radix.** A test degree below `N` derives from `BE::MIN_DEGREE` (both backends' in parity) or a ratio clearing every floor, a case below the floor being skipped or asserted to fail, and derived parameters use struct update over `CKKSTestParams`, keeping `params.base2k`.
- **Host setup math tests.** Host-only setup code (factor generation, Remez, presets, plans) is unit-tested with exact pins.

### 15.4. Fixtures

- **Determinism fixture keys.** A fixture key names only ring, precision, degree and case (`encoding-{ring}-f{bits}-n{n}-slots{s}-..`, `setup-f{bits}-..`), never a backend, and `schemes/poulpy-ckks/src/test_suite/determinism.txt` holds exactly the keys the drivers produce, a diff removing or renaming a key deleting the stale line.
- **Fixture inputs are exact.** Fixture values come from platform-independent inputs (dyadic values, literals, correctly rounded arithmetic) and `CKKSFloat`, serialized little-endian (`scalar_bytes`, `to_le_bytes`), never platform math.
- **Setup-math pins.** A change to `CKKSFloat`, the quantize codecs or the root table keeps `setup_math_golden_vectors` and the fixtures or updates and explains them.
